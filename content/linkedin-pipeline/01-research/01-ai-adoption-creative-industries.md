# AI Adoption Across Creative Industries

## Market Size

- Global AI in Art and Creativity Market: USD 16.6B (2025), projected USD 161.1B by 2034 at 28.7% CAGR
  - Source: https://www.intelevoresearch.com/reports/ai-in-art-and-creativity-market/
- AI in VFX Market: USD 4.87B (2025), projected USD 28.66B by 2035 at 19.46% CAGR
  - Source: https://www.snsinsider.com/reports/ai-in-vfx-market-9394

## Adoption Rates

- 73% of creative professionals believe AI will automate repetitive tasks
- 45% say AI boosts speed and experimentation
- 80% of creative agencies now use AI in some capacity, but only 5% have moved past experimentation
  - Source: Spark AI 2025 industry report
- Gen Z creatives use AI the most but feel the least prepared
  - Source: https://elements.envato.com/learn/ai-trend-report

## Sector Breakdown

### VFX and Animation
- AI used for rotoscoping, compositing, simulation, and rendering on high-budget projects
- Studios treat AI as a workflow accelerator, not a replacement
- Real-time pipelines and virtual production driving fastest growth
  - Source: https://www.vanarts.com/news-article/5-emerging-trends-in-animation-vfx-s-game-art-for-2026/

### Games
- 79% of developers feel positive about AI's impact (Unity 2025 Gaming Report)
- Industry recovering from layoff era but not back to old hiring pace
- Fewer entry points, more emphasis on adaptable skill sets
  - Source: https://www.avgguild.org/post/animation-vfx-game-jobs-in-2026-what-s-changing-and-how-to-stay-hireable

### Advertising
- Global AI spending on marketing and sales: $40B by 2025
- AI powering dreamlike visuals, nostalgia-driven campaigns, bold branding
  - Source: https://business.adobe.com/resources/creative-trends-report.html

## Key Tools (2026)

- **Midjourney**: Standard for rapid idea visualisation in studios and agencies
- **Runway Gen-4**: Film-grade output, adopted by Hollywood studios for pre-vis
- **Sora**: Shut down March 24, 2026 due to computational costs and competitive pressure
- **Alternatives rising**: Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0, Runway Gen-4 now exceed Sora's peak quality
  - Source: https://www.eweek.com/news/sora-alternatives-ai-video-tools-2026/
