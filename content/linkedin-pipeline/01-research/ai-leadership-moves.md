## 2. Meta's VFX Artist Hiring Campaign — AI Training Roles

**The signal:** Meta has been running a targeted recruitment campaign (November 2025 onwards, continuing into 2026) hiring VFX specialists in Canada for three-month contracts focused on AI training and model fine-tuning.

**Nature of roles:** VFX artists reviewing large image datasets, evaluating AI-generated visuals, fine-tuning model outputs. Effectively: creative quality control for AI systems.

**Why this is a leadership signal:** This is a new category of senior-adjacent creative role — VFX artist as AI trainer/evaluator. Meta's recruiters specifically targeted LinkedIn profiles of experienced VFX professionals.

**Industry reaction:** Described by some as "a lifeline and an existential threat simultaneously" — short-term income during an industry downturn, but contributing to building the tools that may reduce long-term VFX demand.

**Source:** https://thelogic.co/news/meta-vfx-hiring-canada-artificial-intelligence/

## 3. AI Leadership Skills Premium — 2026 Data

**What's happening:** Boards and C-suite selection criteria are shifting from "have you done this before?" to "can you translate AI into operating leverage?" Executive recruiters (Talentfoot, March 2026) identify a salary premium for leaders who can:
- Translate AI into operating leverage for their function
- Manage AI risk without freezing progress
- Build organisations that learn faster than the market changes

**Roles most in demand at executive level (2026):**
- AI/ML Engineering leads
- MLOps and production AI leads
- AI Governance and Ethics Specialists
- Forward-Deployed Engineers (AI into client environments)

**Creative-sector specific:** Only 2% of creative teams are described as "fully integrating AI" — leaving significant executive mandate for leaders who can drive this transformation.

**Source:** https://talentfoot.com/ai-leadership-skills-2026/

## 4. UK Creative Sector Hiring Intent — 6-in-10 Planning to Hire

**The signal:** Six in ten businesses in the UK creative sector are planning to hire in the next six months (source: Catchin Talent UK Creative Recruitment Guide, 2026).

**Key trends shaping UK creative hiring:**

## 5. VFX/Animation Workforce Pressure — Structural Shift

**Quantified risk to creative roles (US, from SAG-AFTRA/Animation Guild research):**
- ~21.4% of US film, TV, and animation jobs likely to be consolidated, replaced, or eliminated by GenAI by 2026
- Estimated 118,500 US jobs affected
- California: 39,500 jobs at risk; New York: 15,100

**Most exposed roles cited by industry executives:**
- 3D modellers (one-third of executives predict displacement by 2026)
- Storyboard artists (~15% flagged)
- Surface/material artists (~15% flagged)
- Illustrators and animators (~15% flagged)

**Context on preparedness:** Only 26% of respondents felt their organisation's workforce was "fully prepared" for GenAI integration.

**Source:** https://animationguild.org/ai-and-animation/
