## Top 5 Angles for LinkedIn Posts

### ANGLE 1 — The Pixomondo Signal
**What:** Sony is winding down Pixomondo — the Emmy-winning studio behind House of the Dragon, Star Trek, and Avatar: The Last Airbender. CEO Jonny Slow exited in February. VFX division, virtual production unit (Clara), and R&D arm all closing or being absorbed into Sony Pictures Imageworks (Vancouver).
**Why it works:** This is a structural consolidation story. Sony is abandoning a distributed VP model and concentrating all investment in one studio. The VP market is not growing uniformly — it's bifurcating.
**FPZ angle:** What happens to the talent market when the world's largest entertainment company collapses its VP infrastructure into a single studio? And what does that signal for every other VP studio with a corporate parent?
**Sources:** Televisual, Deadline, Hollywood Reporter (all March 2026)
**Contrarian edge:** While PIER59 just launched the largest LED volume in the Northeast US, Sony is closing one. Growth and consolidation are happening simultaneously in virtual production.

---

### ANGLE 2 — The Pipeline Competence Premium
**What:** Studios are not hiring AI enthusiasts. The Animation/VFX Guild stated explicitly: "Studios are hiring (and promoting) people who can use AI responsibly inside a production pipeline, not people who 'generate cool stuff' in isolation."
**Why it works:** This reframes the entire AI skills conversation. It's not about prompting. It's about pipeline architecture and responsible integration. A talent strategy post disguised as a trend post.
**FPZ angle:** What does an AI-ready VFX/VP hire look like in 2026? And why is the gap between "AI awareness" and "pipeline competence" the biggest talent gap in creative tech right now?
**Sources:** AVG Guild, VFX Voice, Roland Berger

---

### ANGLE 3 — The 37-Point Perception Gap
**What:** IAB (2026) found 82% of ad executives believe consumers feel positive about AI-generated ads. Only 45% of consumers actually do. The gap widened from 32 to 37 points in one year. Gen Z: 39% feel "very or somewhat negative" about AI ads.
**Why it works:** Named source, specific figures, directly uncomfortable for creative leaders. The industry is scaling an AI production strategy based on a wrong read of consumer sentiment.
**FPZ angle:** Are creative directors making production decisions based on a fundamentally flawed assumption about their audiences? And what does this mean for the leaders being hired to scale AI production?
**Contrarian edge:** Meanwhile, Dollar Shave Club, Almond Breeze, and Equinox are using AI to mock AI. The most sophisticated AI advertising strategy in 2026 involves weaponising consumer AI scepticism.
**Sources:** IAB 2026 survey, Digiday, Marketing Brew

---

### ANGLE 4 — UNESCO's 24%
**What:** UNESCO's 2026 report (120+ countries, 8,100+ policy measures) projects music creators will lose 24% of revenue by 2028. Audiovisual workers: 21% income loss. UNESCO Director-General Khaled El-Enany: "This is a critical moment for the creative economy." Disruptions occurring "at a pace that outstrips current policy responses."
**Why it works:** The most credible institutional data on AI's creative economy impact. Specific percentage, specific sector, specific timeline. Not speculative.
**FPZ angle:** When the UN projects 24% revenue drops for creative workers, what are boards and creative leadership teams actually doing about it? And what does responsible AI leadership in creative studios look like right now?
**Source:** UNESCO Re|Shaping Policies for Creativity, February 2026

---

### ANGLE 5 — Adobe x NVIDIA: The Infrastructure Play
**What:** Adobe and NVIDIA's March 16 partnership is not a product update. It's Adobe positioning as the backbone of enterprise creative production automation — agentic workflows, autonomous pipelines, OpenUSD 3D digital twins, CUDA acceleration across every major tool.
**Why it works:** Most commentary treats this as a feature announcement. The bigger story: Adobe is automating the work that boutique studios and creative technologists charge premium rates for.
**FPZ angle:** When the world's largest creative software company builds autonomous production pipelines, what happens to the humans who were the pipeline? And what senior roles survive?
**Contrarian edge:** Adobe is simultaneously democratising at the bottom (unlimited Firefly generations for individuals) and automating at the top (enterprise pipeline automation). It's a market squeeze.
**Sources:** Adobe newsroom, NVIDIA newsroom (16 March 2026)
