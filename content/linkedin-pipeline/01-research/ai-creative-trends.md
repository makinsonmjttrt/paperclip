## 3. AI Video Generation: Market Maturation in Q1 2026

**What's shifting:** The consensus in Q1 2026 is that AI video is moving from generation to orchestration. The key change: image-to-video APIs are outperforming text-to-video in practical production workflows.

**Current tool landscape:**
- Google Veo 3.1 rated best all-around text-to-video model (Zapier, March 2026)
- Runway Gen-4: professional standard for commercial motion graphics, with Director Mode and Motion Brush for precise camera/object control
- Pika 2.5: keyframe animation via Pikaframes; 1080p on paid tiers
- Midjourney Video: superior character consistency, human-like animation preservation
- LTX-2 (local/NVIDIA RTX): 4K video up to 20 seconds, built-in audio, multi-keyframe support — comparable to cloud models running locally

**Key quote from industry analysis (Magic Hour, March 2026):** "The biggest shift heading into 2026 is from generation to orchestration — AI video generation is less about pressing a button and more about directing a system."

**Sources:**

## 5. Gaussian Splatting Hits Production Grade

**What's happening:** 3DGS described by analysts as approaching a "JPEG moment for spatial computing." Timeline: 2023 proved speed, 2024 added geometric accuracy + mobile, 2025 brought standardisation, 2026 = production-grade workflows.

**Specific production examples:**
- Superman (film): first major motion picture to ship with dynamic Gaussian Splatting
- OTOY OctaneRender 2026: full path-traced Gaussian splat support
- Zillow: first major real estate platform to deploy GS via SkyTours feature

**Tools:** Apple's SHARP technology can convert a single photo into a 3D scene in under one second.

**Why it matters for creative production:** Splats are cutting pre-vis and location capture time significantly. Real-time path tracers like Chaos Arena now manage 2 trillion+ polygons for LED stages without crashes.

## 10. Generative AI Advertising — Consumer Backlash vs Creative Director Adoption

**The gap:** IAB data shows 82% of ad executives believe Gen Z/Millennials feel positive about AI-generated ads. Only 45% of consumers actually do. The gap widened from 32 points (2024) to 37 points (2026).

**The shift:** Despite consumer skepticism, creative directors are broadly adopting AI. Digiday (March 2026) reports advertising creatives have "lost the AI ick" — cost savings and legal risk reduction have accelerated adoption regardless of public sentiment.

**Contrarian play:** Brands like Dollar Shave Club, Almond Breeze, and Equinox are now using AI-generated content while simultaneously mocking AI "slop" in their campaigns.

Sources:
- https://www.iab.com/insights/the-ai-gap-widens/
