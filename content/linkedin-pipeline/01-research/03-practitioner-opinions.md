# Real Practitioner Opinions on AI in Creative Work

## VFX Artists

### Prevailing View: Collaboration, Not Replacement
- Most VFX professionals see a symbiotic future: AI handles the grind, artists bring vision and judgment
- AI automates rotoscoping, compositing, simulation tasks
- Artists remain essential for storytelling and creative direction
  - Source: https://wow-how.com/articles/ai-replace-vfx-artists

### Industry Concern: Entry-Level Bottleneck
- Fewer entry points for junior artists as AI handles tasks that used to be training ground
- Studios want "production-ready" from day one
  - Source: https://www.avgguild.org/post/animation-vfx-game-jobs-in-2026-what-s-changing-and-how-to-stay-hireable

## Game Developers

### Unity 2025 Survey: 79% Positive
- Developers largely see AI as augmenting their work
- Strong pushback against full automation of creative process
- "Selling AI as a tool to replace creatives in the pipeline won't cut it. The bulk of the games industry will reject that idea."
  - Source: https://x-team.com/magazine/will-ai-replace-game-developers

### Post-Layoff Reality
- Industry hasn't snapped back to pre-layoff hiring pace
- Companies hiring fewer people but expecting broader skill sets
- Adaptability valued over deep specialism in single tool
  - Source: https://www.avgguild.org/post/animation-vfx-game-jobs-in-2026-what-s-changing-and-how-to-stay-hireable

## Declining Creative Roles (Data)

Among top 10 declining job categories (180M job analysis):
- Computer graphic artists: -33%
- Photographers: -28%
- Writers: -28%
  - Source: https://bloomberry.com/blog/i-analyzed-180m-jobs-to-see-what-jobs-ai-is-actually-replacing-today/

## Film and Video Production

- AI used in pre-production and post-production, less in principal photography
- Crews getting smaller but roles getting more technical
- Labour agreements (SAG-AFTRA, IATSE) shaping how AI can be used on set
  - Source: https://www.productionhub.com/blog/post/will-ai-replace-film-video-production-jobs-in-2026-inside-the-real-industry-shift

## Generational Split

- Gen Z creatives: highest AI adoption, lowest confidence in preparedness
- Senior professionals: carry the business pressure, slower to adopt but more strategic about it
  - Source: https://elements.envato.com/learn/ai-trend-report
