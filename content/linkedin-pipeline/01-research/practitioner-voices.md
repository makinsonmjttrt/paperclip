82% of ad executives believe Gen Z/Millennial consumers feel positive about AI ads. Only 45% of consumers actually do. Gap widened from 32 points in 2024 to 37 points in 2026.
Source: https://www.iab.com/insights/the-ai-gap-widens/

## VFX Industry Analyst Voices

## Contrarian / Surprising Takes Worth Flagging

1. **Brands mocking AI while using it:** Dollar Shave Club, Almond Breeze, and Equinox are all using AI-generated content in campaigns that simultaneously make fun of AI "slop." Source: https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2026/01/28/brands-using-generative-ai-almond-breeze-equinox-dollar-shave-club

2. **Meta as both lifeline and threat:** Meta is paying VFX artists $X/hour (short contracts) to train AI systems — giving income to displaced artists while building the tools that displaced them. Source: https://thelogic.co/news/meta-vfx-hiring-canada-artificial-intelligence/

3. **The pipeline competence premium:** The creative industry labour market is not rewarding AI enthusiasm — it rewards people who can integrate AI into production pipelines. "Generate cool stuff" is worthless. Source: https://www.avgguild.org/post/animation-vfx-game-jobs-in-2026-what-s-changing-and-how-to-stay-hireable

4. **Coca-Cola as cautionary tale:** Fully generative ad pipelines triggered audience backlash when Coca-Cola used AI for its 2025 Christmas ad remake — described as lacking "emotional continuity." Source: https://digiday.com/marketing/why-early-gen-ai-ads-arent-working/
