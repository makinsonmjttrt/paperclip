# Content Gaps
*What nobody in AI recruitment or creative tech is saying on LinkedIn*
*Research period: Last 30 days (March-April 2026)*

---

## The Core Gap

Every AI recruitment firm on LinkedIn talks about AI as a hiring category. Nobody talks about AI as a production reality inside creative industries.

The conversation is dominated by:
- "AI engineers are the fastest-growing job"
- "Chief AI Officers command 67% salary premiums"
- "AI will transform how you hire"

What's completely absent is the story of what's actually happening inside gaming studios, advertising agencies, film VFX houses, experiential production companies, and real-time content teams when AI hits their pipeline.

FPZ lives in that gap.

### Gap 1: The Creative Production Pipeline - Where AI Actually Lands

**What's posted:** Generic "AI is transforming every industry" statements.

**What's missing:**
- Which roles in a creative production pipeline are genuinely disrupted vs. over-hyped
- The sequence in which AI enters: post-production first, then mid-production, now pre-production
- How crew sizes are changing in specific production types (virtual production, brand content, experiential)
- What a production brief looks like in 2026 vs. 2022 - and what that means for the people running the brief

**Why FPZ can own this:** You've placed people inside these pipelines across gaming, film, advertising, experiential. You know where the real change is happening vs. where the LinkedIn narrative is running ahead of reality.
