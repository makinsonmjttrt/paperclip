# Competitor Content Map
*Research period: Last 30 days (March-April 2026)*
*Compiled: 2 April 2026*

---

## Overview

The LinkedIn landscape for AI recruitment content is dominated by two clusters: (1) large tech executive search firms posting about AI engineering leadership, and (2) generalist creative staffing firms posting salary guides. Neither cluster covers the intersection FPZ owns: senior creative talent at the point where AI enters production pipelines.

---

## Competitor Profiles

---

### Christian & Timbers
**Type:** AI-specialist executive search (US-headquartered)
**LinkedIn presence:** Active company page + founder Jeff Christian posts regularly
**Primary audience:** Tech startups, SaaS, enterprise AI teams

**What they post about:**
- Chief AI Officer placement and the rise of the CAIO role
- Embodied AI / robotics moving from research to deployment
- AI as the primary measure of executive performance
- "AI exec search maturity" - how the market has evolved
- Roundup content: "Top AI Executive Search Firms 2026"

**Angles they take:**
- Authority positioning ("we are #1 in AI executive search")
- Market intelligence framing (industry data, trends)
- Speed/precision as differentiators ("searches close in under 6 weeks")

**Sectors covered:** Tech, SaaS, FinTech, cybersecurity, enterprise AI
**Sectors NOT covered:** Creative industries, advertising, gaming, film, experiential

**Engagement notes:** Their roundup content and "top firms" lists generate inbound traffic from SEO more than LinkedIn engagement. Likely moderate engagement on individual posts.

---

### Aquent
**Type:** Large creative staffing firm (global)
**LinkedIn presence:** Active company page, regular content
**Primary audience:** In-house creative teams, marketing departments, brand studios

**What they post about:**
- 2026 Salary Guide (100+ roles, including AI-adjacent creative roles)
- AI Product Manager as highest-paid emerging role ($162,000 median)
- Technical Artist as a role evolving "at the intersection of creative intent and generative models"
- Creative Operations leadership + AI strategy
- AI-powered project management tools (RoboHead Spark launch)
- Pay transparency and skills-first hiring in creative

**Angles they take:**
- Data authority (salary benchmarking is their signature content)
- Practical/operational (here's what's changing in creative workflows)
- Somewhat cautious on AI disruption narrative

**Notable:** Aquent are the closest competitor to FPZ's territory. They cover Technical Artist and Creative Technologist roles. BUT they are a staffing firm (volume-play, contingency), not an executive search firm. Their content treats creative AI roles as data points, not with specialist depth.

**Engagement notes:** Salary guide content consistently performs well. Data-backed posts drive shares.

### Runway (Talent Network - December 2025 launch)
**Type:** AI tool company operating a talent marketplace (NOT a recruiter)
**LinkedIn presence:** Active brand page
**Primary audience:** Brands, studios, and production companies hiring AI filmmakers

**What they're doing:**
- Launched Talent Network in December 2025 - connecting AI-skilled artists with clients
- Already includes studios such as Harmony Korine's EDGLRD
- Positions as a direct-to-talent marketplace, bypassing traditional recruitment

**Why this matters for FPZ:**
- Runway is building infrastructure to disintermediate creative AI recruitment in film/video
- Their talent network is curated around Runway tool fluency - narrow but real
- Does NOT cover executive/leadership placement - creates, not replaces, demand for senior search

**Assessment:** Watch closely. As AI tool companies build talent networks, the mid-tier freelance market may commoditise further. Senior leadership placement (FPZ's territory) becomes more valuable by contrast.

### Industry Voices (Non-Recruitment)

**Shaun Williams (Creative/Marketing)**
- Posts about AI tools for creatives
- Angle: "best AI tools for 2026" - tool reviews, practical tips
- High engagement, candidate/practitioner audience

**James Presbitero (Creator/Educator)**
- Surveyed "AI-ready creators" on what's changing
- Angle: practitioner experience, readiness mindset

**LinkedIn News / Economic Graph**
- "Jobs on the Rise 2026" - AI Engineer tops the list
- Prompt Engineering and AI Engineering now in LinkedIn's "fastest-growing skills" data
- Angle: macro data, trend validation

**Adobe / Runway leadership**
- Adobe-Runway strategic partnership content
- Adobe hiring for AI Experimentation & Adoption roles (LinkedIn job posts active)
- Angle: tool ecosystem partnerships + in-house AI team building
- High reach but platform/brand comms, not career/talent angle

**Envato (State of AI in Creative Work 2026 report)**
- Published major research: nearly 50% of creative professionals use AI daily
- Critical finding: 58% have used AI in client work without disclosing it
- Only 2% of creative teams are fully integrating AI (despite the noise)
- Gen Z leads daily AI use at 54% but only 37% feel prepared for AI-driven industry
- Angle: data authority, industry research
- Engagement: report is widely cited across LinkedIn; creating a second wave of posts referencing it
