"AI experience" is the most overvalued line on a creative brief right now.

I push back on it every week. A client sends a brief asking for "3+ years Midjourney experience." I ask them: Midjourney V5 shipped March 2023. V6 followed in December. The interface and output quality changed so dramatically between versions that V5 workflows were barely relevant by V6.

Same with Runway. Gen-1 to Gen-3 in under 24 months. Different tool each time. Sora went from closed beta to general availability to shutting down. All within the span of a typical probation period.

You cannot spec for experience with tools that reinvent themselves every quarter.

What you can test for is learning velocity. How quickly does someone pick up a new tool, evaluate its output, and build it into a production workflow?

Different question from "how long have you used Midjourney?" Requires a different interview process. One that tests adaptation, not accumulation.

I see briefs every week that confuse familiarity with capability. The two have never been further apart.

How are you testing for AI capability in your creative hiring? Genuine question. I am still refining our approach and want to hear what is working.
