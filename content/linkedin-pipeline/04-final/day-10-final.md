The creative industry does not have an AI problem. It has a leadership problem.

The tools exist. Better than six months ago, worse than six months from now. The talent is findable. I know because finding it is literally what I do.

What is missing is leadership that makes consequential, confident decisions about AI adoption.

Here is the signal I watch for. When I receive an AI creative brief, I look at who authored it. Half the time, it comes from IT or a digital transformation team. Not from creative leadership. Not from the people who understand what the output needs to achieve.

That tells me creative leaders have not taken ownership of AI. When they do not own it, decisions default to whoever moves first. Usually someone with technical authority but no creative judgment.

The result is AI adoption shaped by capability rather than intent. Companies using AI for what it can do rather than what it should do. Brand integrity suffers. Creative standards drift. The team loses confidence.

Strong creative leadership makes deliberate AI choices. Which workflows. Which outputs. Which decisions stay human. That clarity is not a technology decision. It is a leadership one.

If AI adoption at your company is being led by IT rather than creative leadership, the problem is not the tools.

Tell me where I am wrong.
