Three clients have asked me a version of this in the last month. I still do not have a clean answer.

Does your organisation need a Chief AI Creative Officer?

The Chief AI Officer title went mainstream between 2023 and 2025. LinkedIn job data confirms it. But nearly all those roles sit in technology or digital transformation functions. They own AI strategy in the abstract.

What almost no one has is someone who owns the intersection of creative standards and AI capability. Who decides when AI output meets the brand bar. Who knows which creative decisions stay human and which can be machine-assisted. Who builds the workflow connecting the two.

At most companies I work with, that function does not exist. It sits in a gap between the creative director, the CTO, and whoever runs digital transformation. Nobody fully owns it.

Maybe it needs its own title. Maybe it lives inside the creative director role. Maybe it is a VP-level function reporting to the CCO.

I genuinely do not know. What I know is the gap exists and it is getting wider.

If you have solved this, I want to hear how. If you have not, you are in good company.
