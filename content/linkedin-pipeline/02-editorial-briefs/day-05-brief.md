## Topic
AI is creating a barbell salary market in creative roles

## Angle
The salary conversation in AI-era creative hiring is almost always framed as compression (everyone earns less because AI reduces the hours required). The actual picture is a barbell: senior AI-fluent creative talent commanding material premiums; mid-level generalist roles under sustained pressure. This is not a prediction. It is already the pattern in the briefs and offers Martyn handles.

## Key data point
The World Economic Forum's Future of Jobs 2025 report (published January 2025) identified a divergence in skills premium between high-AI-fluency workers and those without. McKinsey's AI adoption surveys have consistently shown higher productivity gains (and corresponding wage impact) concentrated in senior knowledge workers who integrate AI into high-judgement tasks. Neither report is behind a paywall.
