**Post date:** Day 9 (Friday)

**Theme:** Industry trend

## Topic
Everyone is talking about AI eliminating creative jobs. The actual data tells a different story.

## Angle
The displacement narrative dominates every panel, podcast, and press release. The counter-data: LinkedIn's own job posting data shows a material increase in net-new creative-adjacent roles that essentially did not exist in 2022. AI Prompt Engineer. AI Creative Director. AI Production Coordinator. Synthetic Media Producer. The job market is not shrinking - it is reshuffling. And the reshuffling is creating roles that pay well and are chronically hard to fill. That is FPZ's market.

## Key data point
LinkedIn's hiring data consistently shows emerging job categories growing faster than legacy categories are declining in the creative tech space. LinkedIn published data in 2023-2024 showing AI-related job postings as a fast-growing category across industries. The specific new role titles above are observable on LinkedIn job searches - they are documented by presence, not by proprietary research.

## Why this matters
This is the second contrarian post in the sequence. It directly challenges the dominant narrative in a way that is grounded in observable data. For creative leaders who have been anxious about the displacement story, this reframe offers a more useful mental model for talent planning.
