## Topic
Does your organisation actually need a Chief AI Creative Officer?

## Angle
The Chief AI Officer title is proliferating. But in creative organisations, the specific gap is not about AI strategy in the abstract. It's about who owns the intersection of creative standards and AI capability. Most companies have neither the title nor the function. The question is whether they should, or whether this is a job that should live inside an existing role. Martyn uses this as a conversation-starter with no prescribed answer.

## Key data point
Chief AI Officer as a standalone C-suite title went from rare to mainstream between 2023 and 2025. LinkedIn's own job data shows the title appearing in postings across industries. What has not followed at pace is an equivalent role with a creative remit. Observable from LinkedIn job search data.
