## Topic
What WPP, Publicis, and Omnicom's AI restructuring tells us about where in-house creative teams are heading

## Angle
The holding companies restructured first. They always do. What WPP announced under Mark Read and what Publicis did under Arthur Sadoun are not isolated decisions. They are the leading indicators for what brand-side creative directors and heads of content will be asked to do in the next 12-18 months. Martyn reads the agency moves as advance intelligence for his in-house clients.

## Key data point
WPP publicly committed to £300m annual AI investment (announced in 2024 earnings communications). Publicis Groupe announced their "Marcel" AI platform and related workforce changes publicly across multiple earnings calls and press releases. Both companies have publicly discussed restructuring creative production workflows around AI. These are on-record, public statements.
