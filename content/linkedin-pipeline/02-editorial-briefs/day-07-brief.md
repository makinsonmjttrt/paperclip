## Topic
Canva's AI suite is the most important signal in creative hiring that most leaders are ignoring

## Angle
When the most widely used design tool in the world ships a fully AI-assisted creative workflow to 170 million users, it does not just affect Canva users. It resets the baseline expectation for what "entry-level creative" means. Martyn's read: Canva's Magic Studio and AI features are not a consumer novelty. They are a structural reset for where junior creative talent needs to start from.

## Key data point
Canva publicly reports 170+ million monthly active users (stated in various press releases and investor communications through 2024-2025). The Magic Studio AI suite (including AI image generation, Magic Write, AI video tools) launched progressively through 2023-2024 and is built into the free and Pro tiers. This is a documented, publicly announced product suite.

## Source URL
https://www.canva.com/newsroom/ (Canva Newsroom - source for product announcements)
https://www.canva.com/magic-studio/ (Magic Studio product page)

## Why this matters
Creative leaders hiring at any level need to understand that the baseline has shifted. The person entering the creative workforce in 2026 has had AI-assisted design tools as their default for years. This is not a future problem. Martyn can name Canva specifically as a verifiable, concrete anchor.

## Voice note
Mostly Martyn personal voice, occasional FPZ mention. Use Canva as the lens, not the subject. The subject is what the baseline shift means for hiring at every level. "When I look at a brief for a junior creative role, I now assume AI fluency is ambient. Canva made sure of that."
