## Posting rhythm recommendation

Monday / Wednesday / Friday cadence. Avoid posting Saturday-Sunday. Per LinkedIn algorithm research (Richard van der Blom's 600K-post dataset), first-hour engagement velocity drives distribution. Post when Martyn's core audience (creative directors, heads of content, senior designers, HR/talent leads in creative industries) is active: Tuesday-Thursday mornings perform best generally, but the Mon/Wed/Fri cadence maintains consistency without cannibalising individual post performance.
