## Topic
AI skills have overtaken every other category in creative job postings

## Angle
LinkedIn's own Skills on the Rise data shows AI-adjacent capabilities have displaced traditional creative software skills as the top requirement in design, content, and production briefs. This isn't a prediction. It already happened. Martyn's angle: what does this actually mean for how you're writing your hiring specs right now?

## Key data point
LinkedIn's Economic Graph Skills on the Rise reports have consistently ranked AI literacy and generative AI skills among the fastest-growing requirements across creative and marketing roles. The shift from "Photoshop" to "AI workflow" as the primary spec criteria happened faster than most hiring managers anticipated.

## Source URL
https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/research/future-of-work

## Why this matters
Creative industry leaders reading this are either rewriting job specs or should be. This post gives them the data signal they need to justify the change internally, and positions Martyn as the person who reads this market ahead of them.
