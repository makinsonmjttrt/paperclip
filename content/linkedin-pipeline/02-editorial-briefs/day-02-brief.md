## Topic
"AI experience" is the most overvalued line on a creative brief right now

## Angle
Every creative job spec lists the same AI tools. Sora. Midjourney. Runway. Firefly. The problem: by the time you've hired, shortlisted, and onboarded, two of those tools have had major version releases and your spec is already obsolete. The brief should be testing learning velocity, not tool familiarity. This is the contrarian case Martyn makes from what he sees in briefs week to week.

## Key data point
Midjourney has released multiple major versions since 2022. Runway went from Gen-1 to Gen-3 in under 24 months. OpenAI's Sora moved from closed beta to general availability within months. The AI tool landscape has a shorter half-life than a typical hiring cycle (average time-to-hire for senior creative roles runs 60-90 days). Sourcing anchors: company changelogs, launch announcements, observable public record.
