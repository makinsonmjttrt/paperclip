# Day 2 Draft

"AI experience" is the most overvalued line on a creative brief right now.

I push back on it every week. A client sends a brief asking for "3+ years Midjourney experience." I ask them: Midjourney V5 shipped in March 2023. V6 followed in December. The interface and output quality changed so dramatically between versions that workflows built around V5 were barely relevant by V6.

Same story with Runway. Gen-1 to Gen-3 in under 24 months. Completely different tool each time. Sora went from closed beta to general availability to shutting down entirely. All within the span of a typical probation period.

You cannot spec for experience with tools that reinvent themselves every quarter.

What you can test for is learning velocity. How quickly does this person pick up a new tool, evaluate its output, and build it into a production workflow?

That is a different question from "how long have you used Midjourney?" And it requires a different interview process. One that tests adaptation, not accumulation.

I see briefs every week that confuse familiarity with capability. The two have never been further apart.

How are you currently testing for AI capability in your creative hiring? Genuine question. I am still refining our approach and want to hear what is working.
