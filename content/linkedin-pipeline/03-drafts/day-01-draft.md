# Day 1 Draft

The most in-demand skill on creative job specs is no longer Photoshop.

LinkedIn's Skills on the Rise data has been tracking this shift for over a year. AI literacy and generative AI capability now rank above traditional creative software skills in design, content, and production briefs.

This is not a prediction. It already happened.

I read dozens of creative briefs every week. The change was fast. Twelve months ago, the typical senior creative spec led with Adobe suite mastery and five years of brand work. Now the first filter is AI fluency. Can this person build a workflow using generative tools? Can they evaluate AI output against brand standards? Can they move at the speed these tools make possible?

The tools themselves change faster than you can hire. Average time-to-hire for a senior creative role runs 60 to 90 days. In that window, Midjourney ships a new version. Runway launches Gen-4. Sora gets shut down entirely.

So the real question for hiring managers is not which tools someone knows. It is how fast they learn new ones.

If your creative job specs still lead with specific tool names, you are already filtering out the people who would add the most value.

What does AI fluency actually look like on the briefs you are writing right now?
