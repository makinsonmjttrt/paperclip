# Day 4 Draft

Three clients have asked me a version of this question in the last month. I still do not have a clean answer.

Does your organisation need a Chief AI Creative Officer?

The Chief AI Officer title went from rare to mainstream between 2023 and 2025. LinkedIn job data confirms the proliferation. But nearly all of those roles sit in technology or digital transformation functions. They own AI strategy in the abstract.

What almost no one has is a person who owns the intersection of creative standards and AI capability. Who decides when AI output meets the brand bar. Who knows which creative decisions should be human-led and which can be machine-assisted. Who builds the workflow that connects the two.

At most companies I work with, that function does not exist. It sits in a gap between the creative director, the CTO, and whoever runs digital transformation. No one fully owns it.

Maybe it needs its own title. Maybe it lives inside the creative director role. Maybe it is a VP-level function that reports to the CCO.

I genuinely do not know the right answer. What I do know is that the gap exists and it is getting wider.

If you have solved this at your company, I want to hear how. If you have not, you are in good company.
