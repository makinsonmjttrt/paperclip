# Day 3 Draft

WPP committed to 300 million pounds a year in AI investment. Publicis restructured entire production workflows around their Marcel platform. Omnicom is not far behind.

When the holding companies move this fast, it is advance notice for everyone else.

I did not read these announcements as agency news. I read them as a 12-to-18-month preview of what brand-side creative directors and heads of content will be asked to do.

The pattern is consistent. Agencies restructure first because they face margin pressure first. They absorb the disruption, standardise the new workflow, and then their clients ask: why are we not doing this in-house?

Mark Read at WPP has been explicit about AI replacing production hours. Arthur Sadoun at Publicis has talked openly about redeploying human capacity toward strategy and creative judgment. These are on-record statements from investor calls.

If you lead a creative team on the brand side, here is the question worth sitting with: what will your board ask you to implement once these agency workflows become the new normal?

That conversation is coming. The only variable is timing.

I work with both agencies and in-house teams. The smartest leaders on the brand side are already watching these restructuring moves and mapping the implications for their own teams.

What are you seeing from the agencies you work with? Has the conversation started at your end?
