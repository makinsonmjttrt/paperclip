# Day 9 Draft

Everyone talks about AI eliminating creative jobs. The actual data tells a different story.

Open LinkedIn right now and search for these titles: AI Prompt Engineer. AI Creative Director. AI Production Coordinator. Synthetic Media Producer.

None of these roles existed in 2022. All of them pay well. Most of them are chronically hard to fill.

LinkedIn's own Economic Graph data consistently shows emerging AI-adjacent creative roles growing faster than legacy roles are declining. The job market is not shrinking. It is reshuffling.

Here is what I see from the recruitment side. The companies that framed AI as a threat cut headcount. They saved money in the short term. Then they discovered they needed people with skills that did not exist in their old org chart. Now they are hiring again, often at higher salaries, for roles they had no budget line for 18 months ago.

The companies that framed AI as an evolution kept their teams, invested in upskilling, and got to the new capability faster. They are not in a talent crisis. The others are.

Computer graphic artist postings are down 33%. But AI Creative Director roles are up from zero to 277 active listings on Indeed alone. The headline "AI is killing creative jobs" is a lazy read of a more interesting picture.

What new role title exists at your company that was not there two years ago?
