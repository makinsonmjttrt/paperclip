# Day 8 Draft

Five human skills are commanding a salary premium in AI-era creative teams.

I track this through the briefs and offers I handle. These are the capabilities that consistently show up in the best-compensated creative roles right now. Not the obvious ones.

**1. Creative direction under ambiguity**
Making consequential aesthetic decisions with incomplete information. AI generates options. Someone has to choose. Fast. With limited context. That judgment is worth more now than it was two years ago.

**2. Cross-functional translation**
The ability to convert between creative, technical, and commercial language in the same meeting. AI has made creative teams more cross-functional. The person who can bridge those conversations commands a premium.

**3. Taste calibration**
Knowing when AI output meets the brand bar and when it falls short. This is not about personal preference. It is about pattern recognition at scale. Most organisations do not have enough of it.

**4. Stakeholder management at pace**
AI has compressed production timelines. Review cycles that used to take days now take hours. Managing stakeholder input at that speed is a specific skill. Not everyone has it.

**5. Ethical and brand risk judgment**
Knowing what AI should not produce, not just what it can produce. This is the skill with the steepest growth curve in demand.

McKinsey's generative AI research supports the macro trend: productivity gains concentrate in senior workers who integrate AI into high-judgment tasks. These five are where I see that playing out in practice.

Which of these is hardest to assess at interview stage?
