# Day 7 Draft

Canva shipped AI-assisted design to 170 million users. That is not a product update. It is a hiring reset.

Magic Studio put AI image generation, AI writing, and AI video tools inside the free tier of the most widely used design platform in the world. Every marketing coordinator, social media manager, and junior creative now has AI-native design in their default toolkit.

This changes what "entry-level creative" means.

The person entering the creative workforce in 2026 has never known design without AI assistance. Their baseline includes capabilities that were senior-level specialisms three years ago. They can generate concepts, iterate variations, and produce finished assets at a speed that was impossible without a full production team.

For hiring managers, this raises a real question. If your junior creatives arrive with AI fluency baked in, what are you actually testing for at interview stage?

The answer is judgment. Can they tell when the AI output is on-brand and when it is not? Can they direct the tool rather than accept whatever it produces? Do they have the visual literacy to know the difference between good enough and genuinely good?

Canva gave everyone the tools. The differentiator is now what people do with them.

When you look at your junior creative hires, what has actually changed about what you test for?
