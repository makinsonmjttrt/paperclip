# Day 6 Draft

Last quarter we ran a search that changed three times before we placed anyone.

The client came to us looking for a senior motion designer. Solid brief. Clear spec. Budget agreed. We started shortlisting.

Two weeks in, the client's team adopted Runway Gen-4. Suddenly half the motion work that justified the role was handled by AI. The brief shifted to a hybrid creative-technical profile. Someone who could direct AI-generated motion and refine the output rather than build from scratch.

We adjusted the shortlist. Started conversations with a different talent pool.

Three weeks later, the client saw what Runway could do with their brand assets specifically. The role shifted again. Now they wanted someone who could build the entire AI-assisted production pipeline, not just operate within it.

Different role. Different seniority. Different budget.

We placed someone excellent in the end. But the person we placed bore almost no resemblance to the original brief.

This is not a one-off. AI is making creative briefs genuinely unstable. The tools evolve mid-search. What the team can do changes. What they need changes with it.

The recruitment firms and internal talent teams that will thrive are the ones built for this instability. Not the ones still working from a fixed spec.

Has the brief changed under your feet on a recent hire? I am hearing this more and more.
