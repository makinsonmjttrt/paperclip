# Day 10 Draft

The creative industry does not have an AI problem. It has a leadership problem.

The tools exist. They are better than they were six months ago and worse than they will be six months from now. The talent is findable. I know because finding it is literally what I do.

What is missing is leadership that makes consequential, confident decisions about AI adoption.

Here is the signal I watch for. When I receive an AI creative brief, I look at who authored it. At least half the time, it comes from IT or a digital transformation team. Not from creative leadership. Not from the people who understand what the output needs to achieve.

That pattern tells me the creative leaders have not taken ownership of AI. And when they do not own it, the decisions default to whoever moves first. Usually someone with technical authority but no creative judgment.

The result is AI adoption shaped by capability rather than intent. Companies end up using AI for what it can do rather than what it should do. Brand integrity suffers. Creative standards drift. The team loses confidence in the process.

Strong creative leadership makes deliberate AI choices. Which workflows. Which outputs. Which decisions stay human. That clarity is not a technology decision. It is a leadership one.

If AI adoption at your company is being led by IT rather than by creative leadership, the problem is not the tools. The problem is the org chart.

Tell me where I am wrong.
