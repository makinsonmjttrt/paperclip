# Newsletter Snippets
*2-3 sentence summaries for newsletter integration*
*Repurposed from 10-day LinkedIn content pipeline*

## Day 1: AI Fluency is the New Baseline
AI literacy has overtaken traditional creative software skills as the lead requirement on job specs. The real question for hiring managers is not which tools someone knows, but how fast they learn new ones. If your specs still lead with tool names, you are filtering out the highest-value candidates.

## Day 2: "AI Experience" is Overvalued
Midjourney reinvented itself twice in 2023. Runway went through three generations in 24 months. Sora launched and shut down within a probation period. You cannot spec for experience with tools that change quarterly. Test for learning velocity instead.

## Day 3: Holding Companies as a Preview
WPP, Publicis, and Omnicom are restructuring production workflows around AI at speed. Agencies face margin pressure first and standardise before their clients do. Brand-side creative leaders should be watching these moves and mapping the implications for their own teams.

## Day 4: The Chief AI Creative Officer Gap
The Chief AI Officer role went mainstream but sits almost entirely in tech or digital transformation. Nobody owns the intersection of creative standards and AI capability. This gap between creative director, CTO, and digital transformation is widening and most companies have not filled it.

## Day 5: The Salary Barbell
Creative compensation is not compressing uniformly. Senior talent with genuine AI fluency commands material premiums. Mid-level generalist roles face sustained downward pressure. The WEF and McKinsey both confirm this divergence at the macro level.

## Day 6: Briefs in Motion
A real FPZ search changed from motion designer to hybrid creative-technical to AI pipeline architect before placement. AI is making creative briefs genuinely unstable because tools evolve mid-search and reshape what teams need. Firms built for this instability will win.

## Day 7: The Canva Hiring Reset
Canva put AI-native design tools in front of over 170 million users. Entry-level creatives in 2026 have never known design without AI assistance. The interview question shifts from "can they use the tools" to "can they exercise judgment about AI output quality."

## Day 8: Five Premium Skills
The five human skills commanding salary premiums in AI-era creative teams: creative direction under ambiguity, cross-functional translation, taste calibration, stakeholder management at pace, and ethical/brand risk judgment. All centre on high-judgment work that AI cannot replace.

## Day 9: Reshuffling, Not Killing
Computer graphic artist postings are down 33% according to Bloomberry's 180M job analysis. But AI Creative Director, Prompt Engineer, and Synthetic Media Producer roles did not exist in 2022 and are now chronically hard to fill. The creative job market is reshuffling, not shrinking.

## Day 10: The Leadership Problem
The creative industry's real challenge is not AI tools or talent. It is leadership. When AI creative briefs are authored by IT rather than creative leadership, adoption is shaped by capability rather than intent. Creative leaders need to own this space before someone else does.
