# X/Twitter Singles
*One standalone post per day, < 280 chars*
*Repurposed from 10-day LinkedIn content pipeline*

---

**Day 1**
The most in-demand skill on creative job specs is no longer Photoshop. AI literacy now leads the brief. If your specs still list specific tool names, you're filtering out the people who'd add the most value.

**Day 2**
"3+ years Midjourney experience." Midjourney V5 shipped March 2023. V6 in December. The tool reinvented itself twice in that window. You can't spec for experience with tools that change quarterly. Test for learning velocity instead.

**Day 3**
WPP: £300M/year on AI. Publicis: production restructured around Marcel. When holding companies move this fast, it is advance notice for brand-side creative teams. Your board will ask you to implement this. The only variable is timing.

**Day 4**
Three clients asked me this month: do we need a Chief AI Creative Officer? Nobody owns the intersection of creative standards and AI capability. It sits in a gap between the CD, CTO, and digital transformation lead. If you've solved this, I want to hear how.

**Day 5**
Creative salary data shows a barbell, not compression. Senior AI-fluent talent commanding premiums. Mid-level generalist roles under pressure. Your compensation benchmarks from 18 months ago are wrong at both ends.

**Day 6**
We ran a creative search that changed three times before we placed anyone. Started as a motion designer. Ended as an AI pipeline architect. Different role, seniority, and budget. AI is making creative briefs genuinely unstable.

**Day 7**
The person entering creative work in 2026 has never known design without AI assistance. If your juniors arrive with AI fluency baked in, what are you actually testing for at interview? Judgment. Visual literacy. The ability to direct, not just accept.

**Day 8**
Five skills commanding a premium in AI-era creative teams: creative direction under ambiguity, cross-functional translation, taste calibration, stakeholder management at pace, ethical and brand risk judgment. Which is hardest to assess at interview?

**Day 9**
Computer graphic artist postings down 33%. AI Creative Director roles up from zero to hundreds. "AI is killing creative jobs" is a lazy read of a more interesting picture. The market is reshuffling, not shrinking.

**Day 10**
The creative industry does not have an AI problem. It has a leadership problem. When AI adoption is led by IT rather than creative leadership, decisions default to capability rather than intent. That is not a technology decision. It is a leadership one.
