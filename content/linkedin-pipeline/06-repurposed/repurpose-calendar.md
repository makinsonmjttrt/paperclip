## Distribution Timing

**LinkedIn:** Post between 07:30-08:30 UK time (Tuesday-Friday performs best for B2B)

**X/Twitter Thread:** Same day as LinkedIn, staggered by 2-3 hours. Reframe slightly for X audience (shorter, punchier, more conversational).

**X/Twitter Single:** Next morning, 08:00-09:00 UK time. Standalone distillation of the key insight.

**Newsletter:** Compile into two weekly editions:
- **Week 1 (Days 1-5):** "AI fluency, compensation, and the roles nobody's named yet"
- **Week 2 (Days 6-10):** "Brief instability, hiring resets, and the leadership gap"

---

## Content Groupings

### Week 1 Theme: The Market Shift
Posts 1-5 build a connected argument: skills are changing (1-2), agencies are restructuring (3), leadership roles are missing (4), and compensation reflects all of it (5).

### Week 2 Theme: What to Do About It
Posts 6-10 shift from observation to action: briefs are unstable (6), entry-level is reshaped (7), premium skills are identifiable (8), the market is reshuffling (9), and leadership must own the response (10).
