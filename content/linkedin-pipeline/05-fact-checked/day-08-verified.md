Five human skills are commanding a salary premium in AI-era creative teams.

I track this through the briefs and offers I handle. Not the obvious ones.

**1. Creative direction under ambiguity**
Making consequential aesthetic decisions with incomplete information. AI generates options. Someone has to choose. Fast. With limited context. That judgment pays more now than it did two years ago.

**2. Cross-functional translation**
Converting between creative, technical, and commercial language in the same meeting. AI has made creative teams more cross-functional. The person who bridges those conversations commands a premium.

**3. Taste calibration**
Knowing when AI output meets the brand bar and when it falls short. Not personal preference. Pattern recognition at scale. Most organisations do not have enough of it.

**4. Stakeholder management at pace**
AI compressed production timelines. Review cycles that took days now take hours. Managing stakeholder input at that speed is a specific skill.

**5. Ethical and brand risk judgment**
Knowing what AI should not produce, not just what it can. This is the skill with the steepest growth curve in demand right now.

McKinsey's generative AI research supports the macro picture: productivity gains concentrate in senior workers who integrate AI into high-judgment tasks. These five are where I see that playing out in practice.
