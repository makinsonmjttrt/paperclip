Everyone talks about AI eliminating creative jobs. The actual data tells a different story.

Open LinkedIn right now. Search for: AI Prompt Engineer. AI Creative Director. AI Production Coordinator. Synthetic Media Producer.

None of these existed in 2022. All pay well. Most are chronically hard to fill.

LinkedIn's Economic Graph data shows emerging AI-adjacent creative roles growing faster than legacy roles are declining. The job market is not shrinking. It is reshuffling.

Here is what I see from the recruitment side. The companies that framed AI as a threat cut headcount. Saved money short term. Then discovered they needed people with skills that did not exist on their old org chart. Now they are hiring again, often at higher salaries, for roles they had no budget line for 18 months ago.

The companies that framed AI as an evolution kept their teams, invested in upskilling, and reached the new capability faster. They are not in a talent crisis. The others are.

Computer graphic artist postings down 33%. AI Creative Director roles up from zero to hundreds of active listings. "AI is killing creative jobs" is a lazy read of a more interesting picture.

What new role title exists at your company that was not there two years ago?
