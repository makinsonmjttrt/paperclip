Apple's new VP of AI Product Marketing joined last week. The role didn't exist at Apple 18 months ago.

Lilian Rincon spent nine years at Google, building consumer AI communication across Shopping, Assistant, and search. She will own how Apple Intelligence and the rebuilt Siri are communicated to market.

Apple's AI narrative has taken damage over the past year. Missed product commitments. A Siri that didn't deliver what the keynote promised. The hire is a specific fix for a specific problem: Apple needed someone who had successfully communicated consumer AI at scale. Rincon has done that. Her track record was the brief. Her familiarity with Apple's internal culture wasn't part of the criteria.

VP-level AI roles that didn't exist two years ago are appearing across every major technology company now. The talent pool for these inaugural appointments is thin. Most of the people who could fill them have done something adjacent in a different sector, not the same role in the same industry.

The brief has to reflect that. Companies still writing conventional VP job descriptions for genuinely inaugural roles are making the search harder than it needs to be.

What does the briefing challenge look like for inaugural AI leadership roles in your sector?
