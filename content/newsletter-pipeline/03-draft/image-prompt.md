# ImageFX Prompt — April 2026 Newsletter Cover

---

## Primary Prompt

Editorial cover image for a LinkedIn article. Muted, executive palette: deep charcoal, warm ivory, and a single accent of antique gold. Abstract geometric composition showing four distinct nodes connected by fine lines converging toward a central point — suggesting executive appointments, acquisitions, and strategic moves rather than technology. Clean negative space in the upper third for article title text. No faces, no people, no company logos. No bright colours. No tech-startup energy. The aesthetic is boardroom-level, understated confidence. Think Financial Times weekend supplement meets a premium executive search firm's identity.

Style: flat geometric editorial, minimal shadows, matte finish. Ultra-wide format suitable for LinkedIn article banner (1920x1080).

---

## Alternate Prompt (if primary produces anything too abstract)

A clean top-down aerial view of a sparse executive desk. Antique ivory surface. Four small identical objects — perhaps sealed envelopes or small weighted blocks — arranged in a subtle diamond formation, connected by a single thin gold thread. Deep charcoal background beyond the desk edge. Upper third of the frame is empty for title placement. Photorealistic but minimal. No clutter. No faces. The mood is deliberate, private, high-stakes. Like the moment before a board decision.
