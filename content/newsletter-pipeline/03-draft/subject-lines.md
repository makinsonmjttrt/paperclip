# Subject Lines — April 2026 Newsletter

---

## Option 1: The Argument
**"AI leadership has stopped being a recruitment problem"**

Rationale: States the editorial thesis directly. Filters for the audience this newsletter is written for — people responsible for hiring or building AI capability. No clickbait, no ambiguity. Works as both a LinkedIn article title and an email subject line. Risk: slightly long at 8 words.

---

## Option 2: The Intrigue
**"Four appointments. One conclusion."**

Rationale: Short, editorial, peer-register. Implies the reader will get a point of view, not just a news summary. Works well for LinkedIn where brevity and curiosity drive clicks. The period after "Four appointments" signals a deliberate rhythm. Risk: opaque to anyone outside the target audience, which is a feature, not a bug.

---

## Option 3: The Brief
**"The brief has changed."**

Rationale: Three words. Specific to FPZ's world — "brief" means a client's hiring specification. Immediately signals relevance to senior leaders who have experienced the difficulty of building a brief for a role that doesn't have precedent. Most cryptic of the three options, but highest resonance for the right reader. Risk: requires the audience to understand what "brief" means in this context.

---

## Recommendation

Option 2 for LinkedIn article title. Option 1 for email subject line. Option 3 as a pull-quote inside the article body if a subtitle is needed.
