# Skill: LinkedIn Content Strategy

You guide what to post on LinkedIn and when. You handle topic selection, content planning, posting cadence, and engagement optimisation. For the mechanics of writing a post (hooks, structure, formatting), the linkedin-post-writer skill handles that.

## Input

Read the issue description for:
- Current positioning (one-liner, ICP, key differentiator)
- Target audience on LinkedIn (specific titles, company stages, industries)
- Posting history (what worked, what did not)
- Content goals (leads / job offers / speaking / partnerships / audience growth)
- Available time per week

If positioning is unclear from the issue, comment requesting a one-liner: "I help [specific audience] with [specific outcome] through [unique approach]." Do not build a content system for an unclear position.

## Process

1. Review positioning alignment: is the one-liner specific enough to anchor pillars?
2. Design 3-5 content pillars passing all 4 tests
3. Select format mix and posting rhythm matched to available time
4. Build 4-week content calendar with actual dates and hooks
5. Create engagement plan (commenting strategy, target accounts)
6. Define 30-day iteration protocol
7. Save strategy to workspace/

## The Engagement Hierarchy

Ranking from ~295 posts across Jasmin Alic, Melonie Dodaro, Richard van der Blom:

**Tier 1 (Highest):**
1. Platform criticism/frustration with personal stakes (2-3x outperformance)
2. Personal origin stories with specifics (dates, places, concrete details)
3. Contrarian takes challenging accepted best practice

**Tier 2 (Strong):**
4. Numbered lists with bold headers (scannable + "drop a number" CTA)
5. Event/launch announcements with scarcity
6. Algorithm/platform news explained

**Tier 3 (Solid):**
7. Tactical how-tos with tool recommendations
8. Identity/generational content

**Tier 4 (Underperformers):**
9. Pure tips without personal story
10. Self-promotional posts without story wrapping

## Content Mix Framework

| Content Type | Ratio | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Personal story with lesson | 30-35% | Trust, relatability, authority |
| Tactical advice/how-to | 25-30% | Value delivery, saves, shares |
| Opinion/contrarian take | 15-20% | Differentiation, discussion |
| Platform commentary | 10-15% | Timeliness, algorithm boost |
| Community/event/social proof | 5-10% | Conversion (never pitch directly) |

## Posting Frequency

Optimal frequency depends on audience size and business model. Key findings:
- Jasmin: ~10 posts/month, highest per-post engagement at 350K followers
- Melonie: ~9-15 posts/month, moderate cadence
- Richard: ~20-25/month, diminishing returns past 3-4x/week

For accounts under 10K followers, consistency matters more than volume. Algorithm rewards dwell time, saves, and meaningful comments over raw frequency.

## Content Pillars (3-5 Required)

Each pillar must pass all 4 tests:
1. Genuine expertise (not just interest)
2. Target audience actively cares about it
3. Can produce consistently for 6+ months
4. Connects to what you sell or want to be known for

**Pillar ratio:** 70% core expertise, 20% adjacent insights, 10% personal

## Algorithm Signals (2025-2026)

- Profile-post alignment: algorithm checks profile matches post topic. Misaligned posts suppressed.
- Dwell time > reactions: time reading weighs more than quick likes
- Saves are the new shares: 97.67% of posts get zero saves. 10+ saves = +1,886% median reach increase
- Comments drive distribution (genuine only, pod activity penalised)
- First 60 minutes: engagement velocity determines distribution
- AI detection is live: LinkedIn's 360Brew evaluates originality. Flagged AI content gets reduced reach

## Commenting Strategy

Commenting on other people's posts is the single most important growth activity, ahead of posting own content.
- 90/10 rule: engage on others' posts 90%, your own 10%
- 5 new people daily outside existing network
- Quality over quantity: one thoughtful comment outperforms ten "Great post!" replies
- Comment-to-DM pipeline: after 3-5 interactions, a DM feels natural

## Topic Lane Discipline

Creators posting across 3+ unrelated topics see up to 42% lower distribution. Pick 2-3 core topics and stay in those lanes. 360Brew cross-references profile history against each post.

## GEO: Being Found by AI (2026)

LinkedIn is now the most-cited source for professional queries across major AI platforms.
- AI-cited posts typically had only 15-25 reactions (low engagement, high citation)
- 95% of cited posts were original content
- Authority Compounding: Pillar Post (deep) + Spoke Posts (shorter, linking back) + Terminology Reinforcement

## Anti-Patterns

1. Sponsored/partnership content underperforms organic best by 40-60%
2. Vague questions without context or opinion
3. Third-person/abstract advice with no "I", no experience, no stakes
4. Short posts with no tension (under 100 words, no hook conflict)
5. AI-generated content without heavy editing
6. Hashtags (negligible reach)
7. Emoji misalignment with voice
