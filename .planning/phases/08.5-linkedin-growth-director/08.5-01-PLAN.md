    - Market: Specialist recruitment for creative production + emerging technology intersection.
    - Practice: CreativAI -- AI-focused arm of FourPointZero's recruitment business.
    - Positioning: "Informed industry observer who understands the intersection" -- NOT "AI experts." Leverage proven creative tech network depth while showing insight into how AI changes creative workflows.
    - Audience: Senior leaders in creative technology companies, AI specialists, decision makers in spatial computing, experiential marketing, interactive exhibitions, immersive design, virtual production, VFX.
    - Brand voice: Professional but approachable. Knowledgeable without being preachy. Direct. Conversational, industry-insider perspective. Short sentences.
    - Language: UK English always. Never use: staffing, temp, body shop, resources (for people), disruptive, synergy, leverage.
