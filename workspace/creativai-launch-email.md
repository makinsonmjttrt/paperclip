## Email Body

Studios come to us with a specific problem: find someone who can run AI-integrated production at a senior level. Not AI credentials grafted onto a production title. Someone who has actually done it.

The candidates who fit that description are a small group. They are not posting CVs or responding to InMails. Most are employed, largely passive, and reachable only through direct relationships built over time. That is the search problem CreativAI was built to solve.

The difficulty is that the AI side and the production side exist as separate talent markets. AI specialist recruiters understand credentials but not studios. VFX specialist recruiters know the sector but cannot evaluate AI delivery under real production constraints. Shortlists built from one side fail when they hit the other. You find that out three interviews in.

---

Today, FourPointZero officially launches CreativAI.

It is our dedicated executive search practice for senior AI leadership in creative production. We place the people who run AI-integrated pipelines at VFX studios, media owners, creative software companies, and AI-native platforms.

We have been placing senior talent at this intersection since 2019. Not since AI became a category on job descriptions. Before that. The relationships with Tier 1 candidates at ILM, DNEG, Framestore, Adobe, and Foundry took six years to build. That is the network CreativAI is built from.

What this means in practice: a search firm that can assess both sides. AI delivery and production delivery. A shortlist of people who have shipped real work in Unreal, Nuke, Houdini, and ComfyUI under actual production pressure, and who can lead a team through a workflow that is still changing.

If you have a senior role open now, or one you expect in the next six months, the time to start that conversation is before the mandate is formally live.
