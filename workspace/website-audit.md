# FourPointZero Website Audit

**Conducted:** 3 April 2026
**Auditor:** UX Researcher (FOU-19)
**Properties reviewed:** fourpointzero.io (homepage, about, jobs, talent/candidate, sectors, contact, news)
**Benchmark:** product-marketing-context.md — brand positioning, target audience, brand voice

---

## Summary

The website has the right structure but delivers the wrong message. It reads as a generalist XR/creative tech recruiter that has added AI. It does not read as a specialist AI-in-creative-production executive search practice. The gap between the intended positioning (product-marketing-context.md) and what visitors actually see is significant — and costs conversions at every stage.

---

## 1. Homepage

### What it says

**Headline:** "The Recruiters for Creative Production"
**Subheading:** "For production studios and creative agencies delivering experiential, interactive, spatial computing and real-time projects, where AI is entering production pipelines"
**CTA:** "Read More" (links to About Us)

### What is missing

**The AI intersection advantage is buried.** The headline is generic — any XR/creative recruiter could use it. The subheading relegates AI to a subordinate clause at the end of a long list. A prospective client who needs to hire a CAIO or VP AI Production reads this and does not immediately know FPZ can uniquely solve their problem.

**No conversion path for clients above the fold.** The only CTA is "Read More," directing visitors to the About page. There is no invite to start a search, discuss a brief, or make an enquiry. For a firm that runs on inbound retained search leads, the homepage converts nothing directly.

**No social proof above the fold.** The 89% retention rate, 89% repeat client rate, and 300+ placements are compelling figures. None appear on the homepage. First-time visitors get no credibility signals.

**The candidate/client split is unclear.** The page serves both audiences without directing either. A hiring client and a job-seeking candidate land on the same experience with the same messaging and the same CTA.

### Brand voice gap

**Current copy reads as:** politely corporate, feature-listing, audience-describing.
**Brand voice spec says:** direct, short sentences, insider perspective, speak *to* the audience not *about* them.

On-brand benchmark from product-marketing-context.md:
> "The shift to real-time production is accelerating faster than most studios are hiring for. We know who's ahead of it."

Current copy:
> "For production studios and creative agencies delivering experiential, interactive, spatial computing and real-time projects, where AI is entering production pipelines"

## 2. About Page

### Strengths

- Statistics are strong: 300+ roles, 76% senior placements, 89% 18+ month tenure, 87% roles filled, 89% repeat clients, 84% first-interview success rate.
- Mentions AI explicitly: "AI is entering production pipelines alongside established processes."
- The meta title ("Specialists in AI talent for creative tech") is better than the homepage.

### Issues

**The statistics lack context.** "300+ roles filled" since when? Over what period? Compared to what baseline? The numbers are compelling but float without anchors that make them meaningful to a sceptical senior buyer.

**The AI sentence is passive.** "AI is entering production pipelines alongside established processes, but consistent output is still hard to achieve" describes a problem without claiming capability to solve it. It reads as observation, not proposition.

**No founding narrative.** The product-marketing-context.md establishes six years of network building as a key differentiator. The About page does not tell this story — why FPZ was founded, what unique perspective Martyn brings, or what drives the focus on this intersection.

**No client names or testimonials.** Understood this may be a deliberate choice for confidentiality. However, even attributed quotes from anonymous "VFX studio VFX Supervisor" or "AI-native startup CEO" would build credibility without naming clients.

## 3. Jobs Page

### What is there

12-13 active permanent roles. Mix includes: AV/LED Media Systems Manager (£70-75k), Unreal C++ Developer (£55-65k), 3D Environment Artist (£40-45k), Creative Director (£85-90k), Gen AI Innovation Specialist VFX (£80-120k).

### The positioning contradiction

This is the most acute conversion issue on the site. The positioning document states FPZ is an executive search practice placing senior and C-suite leaders. The jobs page leads with mid-market technical roles (3D artists, Unreal developers) at salary levels that do not signal retained executive search.

A client considering FPZ for a CAIO hire (potential fee: £27,500–£90,000) lands on the jobs page and sees roles more consistent with a developer recruitment agency. This creates category confusion and undercuts premium trust.

There is no separate section, label, or page for executive/senior leadership search. The "Explore Roles" navigation item leads directly into a job board view with no executive filter.

**The domain fragmentation compounds this:** jobs.fourptzero.com appears to operate as a separate application portal. Two domains dilute SEO authority and create a fractured candidate experience.

## 4. Navigation and Site Architecture

### Current navigation

About Us / What We Deliver / Where We Work / Talent / Explore Roles / News / Contact Us

### Issues

**"What We Deliver" does not have a visible URL in search results** — likely mapped to a homepage section rather than a standalone page. This loses SEO value.

**"Where We Work" is the sectors page** (creative-tech-sectors/). The label is vague; search engines and users do not connect "Where We Work" with "sector expertise."

**"Talent" is ambiguous** — it reads as a page *about* FPZ's talent access, but it is actually the candidate experience page. Candidates may not self-identify here. Clients may not find their entry point.

**No CreativAI page exists.** The CreativAI practice is the growth priority for 2026 (target: 30% of revenue). There is no dedicated page for it, no URL, no SEO presence for AI-specific executive search. This is the single largest structural gap on the site.

**No client-side landing page.** Hiring clients have no dedicated entry point ("Start a Search" or "Hire Through FPZ"). The contact page is generic.

**No process/how-it-works page.** For clients new to retained executive search, there is no explanation of what happens after they make contact — the retained model, the container search option, what a typical engagement looks like. This is a conversion blocker for buyers unfamiliar with executive search.

## 5. SEO Coverage Gaps

### Target terms not covered by dedicated pages

| Target Keyword Cluster | Status |
|------------------------|--------|
| Executive search creative production | No dedicated page |
| AI leadership recruitment | No dedicated page |
| CAIO recruitment / Head of AI recruitment | No page |
| Creative technology executive search | Partially covered via About meta, not page content |
| VFX AI talent acquisition | Mentioned on sectors page but not targeted |
| VP AI Production recruitment | No page |
| CreativAI | No page |
| AI production salary guide 2026 | No page (zero presence) |
| Spatial computing executive search | Partially covered on sectors page |

### Technical/structural SEO issues

**Schema markup:** No evidence of structured data for JobPosting (would help jobs page ranking), Organization schema, or LocalBusiness schema for the UK and US offices.

**Domain split:** fourpointzero.io (main site) vs jobs.fourptzero.com (application portal). Split authority dilutes overall domain strength. All job listings should reside under the primary domain.

**Meta title inconsistency:** Homepage title appears to be brand-only; About page has "Specialists in AI talent for creative tech" (stronger); Contact page has "Specialists in AI talent acquisition for..." These should align to a consistent structure.

**Content depth:** News articles cover relevant topic territory but are written at a general industry level. None demonstrate proprietary insight, original data, or the named perspective of a practitioner. Articles do not rank for high-intent commercial queries.

**No salary/market guide content.** A "Creative Production AI Leadership Salary Guide 2026" page would target high-intent client and candidate searches while establishing thought leadership. Zero competitors produce this for the creative production AI intersection.

## 6. Content and Thought Leadership

### News articles reviewed

Topics include: portfolio paradox, AI creative roles without titles, reverse brain drain (gaming to AI), creative industry AI blame game, pixel streaming and AR, AI in virtual production.

### Gap analysis

The topics are on-brand territory. However:

- Articles are written from a general industry observer perspective, not from the specific FPZ practitioner viewpoint.
- No articles are bylined to Martyn Makinson as a named voice.
- No articles feature original data (survey results, salary figures, placement trends).
- None make a direct case for why FPZ's network is uniquely positioned to solve the stated challenge.
- The thought leadership podium on "AI leadership in creative production" is, per the product-marketing-context.md, entirely vacant. The content strategy needs to fill it aggressively — not informational articles but opinion pieces, market takes, and data-backed reports.

## 7. Candidate Experience Page

### Strengths

- Three-tier structure (C-Suite, Senior Leadership, Contract/Interim) is clear and appropriate.
- "We're selective about the companies we approach" signals quality positioning.
- CTA is warm and specific: "If you're considering a move or want a clearer view of the market, let's talk."
- 7-year tenure stat appears here.

### Issues

- The C-Suite section does not mention AI leadership specifically. A passive Tier 1 AI-in-production candidate lands here and sees generic senior placement language.
- No evidence of the proprietary candidate map or the specific AI-creative production intersection.
- Contact is phone/email only — no structured initial engagement form or "register interest" mechanism.
