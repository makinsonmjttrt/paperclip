## Quick Wins — 3 Zero-Budget Actions This Week

These can be executed before Issue 1 publishes. Each one creates infrastructure that compounds.

**1. Update LinkedIn profile with Signal CTA today.**
Add Signal signup link to the Featured section (as the first item) and to the About section close. The About CTA should read something like: *"I publish Signal — a short industry intelligence briefing for creative technology leaders. [Subscribe here]."* Martyn's profile has ~5K+ connections in this exact audience. This turns existing traffic into subscribers with one edit.

**2. Send 20 direct personal messages before Issue 1 goes out.**
Write a single message (not a broadcast, not a LinkedIn blast — individual messages): *"I've been writing a newsletter about AI and creative production hiring. Issue 1 goes out [date]. Thought you might find it useful — here's the link to subscribe."* Start with the 20 people who would immediately understand why Signal exists: former clients, placed candidates, senior network contacts. These 20 should convert at 70–80%. This is the fastest path to the first 15–20 subscribers.

**3. Add a signup CTA to your email signature.**
Every email Martyn sends for the next 30 days is a distribution channel. One line: *"Subscribe to Signal → [link]."* No explanation needed. The name and the brevity do the work.

## Channel Breakdown

### LinkedIn Posts (ongoing)
**Mechanism:** Each Signal issue generates two LinkedIn posts — one announcing the issue, one extracting a single insight as a standalone post. The second type outperforms the first in both reach and subscription conversion. Do not lead with "subscribe to my newsletter." Lead with the insight.

**Expectation:** LinkedIn will drive Tier B subscribers throughout the 30 days. Each post that performs (5+ comments) will generate 3–8 profile visits from new people. The subscribe CTA in the profile converts some of them.

### Direct Invites (personal outreach)
**Mechanism:** Individual messages to Tier A contacts. Not a mail merge. Not a LinkedIn broadcast. Not a group message. One person, one message, in your own voice.

**Volume:** 80–100 messages over 4 weeks. This is the CEO's primary subscriber acquisition task for the month. It is not delegatable.

**Expectation:** 60–75% conversion from people who open and read the message. The drop-off is people who don't open, not people who read and decline.

### Website Signup CTA
**Mechanism:** Beehiiv signup form embedded or linked from fourpointzero.com homepage and creativai.io. Install in week 3 once the product has been validated by initial subscribers.

**Expectation:** Low volume in month 1 (5–10 subscribers). Sets up compounding growth from month 2 onwards as organic traffic grows.

### Personal Network Outreach
**Mechanism:** Covered under Direct Invites above. The distinction worth noting: beyond the structured outreach list, mention Signal in any relevant conversation during the month. Client calls, candidate conversations, industry events. One verbal mention converts better than three LinkedIn posts.

## Referral Loop

Keep this simple in month 1. One mechanism, one ask.

**The ask (to be added to Issues 3 and 4):**

*"If Signal is useful, forward it to one person who's hiring or placing in this space. That's the only way this grows."*

That's it. No incentive. No referral code. No points system. The audience Signal is targeting is senior enough that they respond to directness and won't engage with mechanics that feel like a consumer product.

**Why this works:** A senior VFX supervisor forwarding Signal to the studio's head of production is a higher-quality subscriber acquisition than any paid channel. The forward is an implicit endorsement. Beehiiv's referral tracking will show which issues drove forwards — use that data in month 2 to understand which content earns referrals.

**Month 2 upgrade:** Once you have 50+ subscribers, consider adding Beehiiv's built-in referral feature (subscribers earn a credit for referrals). Evaluate in the week 4 review based on whether the audience engages with that kind of mechanic. At this stage, assume they don't need the incentive — the content should be doing the work.

## 30-Day Review Triggers

At the end of week 4, review these before planning month 2:

| Metric | Review action |
|--------|--------------|
| Open rate on all 4 issues | If consistently above 45%, the audience is engaged — double down on volume. If below 35%, review subject lines and content angle before growing the list. |
| Subscriber source breakdown (Beehiiv shows this) | Identify which channel drove the most subscribers. In month 2, invest more time in the top two channels. |
| Which LinkedIn post drove the most profile visits | That post format and topic territory is the growth template for month 2 LinkedIn content. |
| Direct outreach conversion rate | If below 50%, review the message. If above 70%, extend the outreach list. |
| Any inbound enquiries generated | Note whether any subscriber converted to a client or candidate conversation. This validates the BD case for Signal and informs what content angle to prioritise. |

## What the First 100 Subscribers Unlock

Getting to 100 subscribers is not the end goal — it is the credential. A 100-person list with 45%+ open rates in a niche B2B vertical is a meaningful signal. It is enough to:

- Justify pitching Signal to industry event organisers as a media partner (small but effective for credibility)
- Reference in BD conversations as a proof point for FPZ's market intelligence positioning
- Use open rate data as evidence for the content decisions in month 2
- Begin a more systematic LinkedIn-to-Signal conversion strategy using paid LinkedIn posts (small budget, high targeting precision)

The 100-subscriber milestone is the starting line for the growth phase, not the finish line.
