## Format 1: Two-liner (for LinkedIn / social / email signature)

**Template:**
[Studio type] needed a [role] who could [specific capability]. We delivered a shortlist in [timeframe]. [Candidate] is still in post [time period later].

**Example:**
A UK VFX studio needed a Head of AI Production who understood both the pipeline and the creative floor. We delivered a shortlist in three weeks. The placed candidate is still in post 18 months later.

---

## Format 2: Story version (500-600 words — for LinkedIn articles / BD presentations)

### Context

[Describe the client — type, scale, what they make, where AI sits in their priorities. No named details without consent. Use sector/type identifiers: "a UK VFX studio", "a post-Series C immersive tech company", "a broadcast network's virtual production arm".]

### The challenge

[What was the hiring problem? Be specific about why it was hard:
- How long had the role been open?
- What had they already tried (internal TA, other recruiters, network)?
- What was wrong with the shortlists they'd seen?
- What was the unspoken requirement that previous searches missed?]

### The FPZ approach

[What did FPZ do differently? Be specific:
- What signals did we use to define the right profile?
- How did we reach candidates who weren't responding to other approaches?
- What made our outreach land where others hadn't?
- Did we challenge the brief? If so, how and why?]

### The result

[Directional outcomes only — no unverifiable stats:
- Speed: shortlist delivered in [X weeks]
- Quality signal: [candidate metric — interviewed all/most of shortlist, made offer to first choice, etc.]
- Retention: [candidate still in post X months/years later — if known]
- Client feedback: [quote if available — flag as representative and unconfirmed until contact confirms]]

### Candidate perspective

*[Quote from placed candidate — flag as representative and unconfirmed until contact confirms]*

> "[What the role meant to them. Why they engaged with FPZ's approach. What made this opportunity different from the noise they usually receive.]"

*Note: Confirm this quote directly with the candidate before publishing. Use "[Quote to be confirmed]" until verified.*

## Format 3: Full case study (800-1000 words — for website / pitch deck / press)

[Expand Format 2 with:]
- More context on the client's strategic situation
- Detailed explanation of FPZ's methodology for this specific search
- Candidate background (anonymised) and why they were right for the role
- What the client relationship looked like before and after placement
- Forward-looking note on what FPZ expects to do next with this client

## Self-Critique Checklist

Before passing to Technical Writer, confirm:

- [ ] All identifying details removed or have explicit written consent for publication
- [ ] All outcome claims are directional, not statistical (unless verified and sourced)
- [ ] Client quote flagged as [To be confirmed] if not yet verified with contact
- [ ] Candidate quote flagged as [To be confirmed] if not yet verified with candidate
- [ ] UK English throughout
- [ ] No em-dashes
- [ ] Format 2 (story version) is ready for LinkedIn/sales use immediately after quality gate pass
- [ ] Format 1 (two-liner) is ready to copy-paste into outreach or LinkedIn comments
