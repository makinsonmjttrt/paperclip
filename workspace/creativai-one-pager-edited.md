# CreativAI: executive search for AI leadership in creative production

## What we do

CreativAI is FourPointZero's dedicated AI-sector executive search practice. We place the senior leaders who integrate AI into production pipelines at VFX studios, media owners, creative software companies, and AI-native platforms.

The role profile we specialise in crosses two talent markets at once: AI/ML technical depth on one side, creative production credibility on the other. Most organisations discover this when the role has been open for three months and every shortlist fails on one dimension or the other. Internal TA can reach one pool. Generalist recruiters work from title. VFX specialist recruiters have the production depth but not the AI leadership capability. None of them has both.

## Who we serve

We work with creative software platforms (Foundry, Maxon, SideFX), AI-native companies scaling their operating leadership past Series C, major VFX studios making dedicated AI leadership appointments (DNEG, Framestore), media owners and broadcasters at VP level, and advertising holding company brands creating their first CAIO or Head of AI Strategy roles.

At the most senior level, the candidate pool across all these sectors is roughly 50–75 people globally. They are not responding to generic InMail. Most are not actively looking. Finding them requires knowing who they are before the mandate opens.
