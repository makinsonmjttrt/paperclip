## LinkedIn Post 1 — The candidate side

The senior AI roles in creative production need two things from one person. AI/ML depth and production credibility.

Not many people have both. The ones who do are employed, not looking, and reachable only through relationships built over years. They're at ILM, DNEG, Framestore, Adobe, Foundry. And some smaller studios doing serious work outside the obvious names.

Globally, the realistic candidate pool for a role like Head of AI Production or CAIO at an established studio is around 50 to 75 people.

They hear from recruiters constantly. Most of those calls go nowhere, because the recruiter can't assess them properly.

That's the gap CreativAI is built for.

## LinkedIn Post 2 — The client pain point

The brief I've had most often this year:

Senior AI leadership for creative production. Pipeline ownership. Has to hold credibility with both the creative and technical side. Director level and above.

You start the search. AI recruiters send engineers with no production context. Production recruiters send credible EPs who've never touched an AI pipeline. Months pass. The brief gets rewritten to fit what's available.

The candidates exist. The problem is you need years of working both sides of this market to reach them. Most firms are working one.

That's what we built CreativAI for.
