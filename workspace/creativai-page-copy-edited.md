# AI executive search for creative production

## What is CreativAI?

**CreativAI** is FourPointZero's AI executive search practice. We place the senior leaders who integrate AI into production pipelines at VFX studios, post-production houses, experiential agencies, virtual production facilities, and creative software companies.

The candidate profile is specific: AI/ML technical depth combined with creative production credibility. Most organisations find this search harder than expected when the role has been open three months and every shortlist fails on one dimension or the other.

FourPointZero has been building the candidate network at this intersection since 2019.

## Who we work with

We work with organisations that have an active or imminent mandate for a senior AI leadership role.

**VFX studios and virtual production facilities.** Established studios with confirmed AI transformation programmes. The roles typically sit at the boundary of production pipeline ownership and AI capability delivery.

**Creative software platforms.** Software tool vendors with active AI product development mandates at board level: Foundry, Maxon, SideFX, Adobe.

**AI-native creative companies.** Platforms past Series C that need operating executives with production fluency. These organisations have capital; what they need is a senior leader who understands how creative work actually gets made.

**Media owners and broadcasters.** VP-level mandates, often with confidentiality requirements: BBC Studios, Netflix, broadcasters building AI creative labs or production technology programmes.

**Advertising holding company brands.** First-ever CAIO or Head of AI Strategy appointments at WPP, Publicis, Dentsu brands.

The qualifying bar: established companies with a confirmed AI mandate at board or executive level. Typically 50+ employees, budget for a senior appointment (£150K+ total compensation), active search underway.

We are not the right partner for junior or mid-level hiring, early-stage companies without an operational production function, or organisations still at the AI strategy stage with no hiring need.

---

## What makes this intersection hard to hire for

The senior AI leadership roles in creative production draw from two separate talent markets simultaneously. Generalist executive search firms source on title. Pure AI recruiters have no creative production network. VFX specialist recruiters are building the AI side reactively.

The Tier 1 candidate pool for these roles globally numbers roughly 50 to 75 people. They are not on job boards. Most are not actively looking. Many are contacted weekly by recruiters who cannot assess their actual profile.

Reaching them requires knowing who they are before the mandate opens, and approaching with enough sector context that the conversation is worth having. That is not something you build in response to a brief.

## How CreativAI approaches it

**Network built over six years**

FourPointZero has placed senior creative production talent since 2019 across VFX, spatial computing, real-time, virtual production, and experiential. The relationships with the senior AI-in-production candidates at ILM, DNEG, Framestore, Adobe, and Foundry pre-date this practice. When we approach a candidate, they take the call.

**Production-context evaluation**

These candidates have shipped AI under real production constraints: ComfyUI, Unreal Engine, Nuke, Houdini, Maya under deadline pressure. Title and credentials are not a reliable proxy for this. We assess for production-context delivery, not just seniority.

**Brief review before search**

Searches start with a structured brief: the role mandate, the production context, the team structure, the decision-making environment, and what success looks like in the first 12 months. If the brief will not find the right person, we say so before the search begins.

**Retained search, structured process**

CreativAI works on a retained basis. Typical timeline: 8 to 12 weeks from signed brief to shortlist of three to five qualified candidates. We manage the interview process and support offer negotiation.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is CAIO hiring and why is it specialist work?**

CAIO appointments in creative production companies are typically first-ever senior AI leadership hires. The brief usually requires a candidate who can operate at C-suite level, translate AI capability into production reality, and build credibility with both technical and creative leadership. That profile sits at the intersection of two separate talent markets. Standard executive search methods do not reliably produce qualified shortlists for it. We focus exclusively on this intersection.

**What is creative tech AI recruitment?**

Creative tech AI recruitment is executive search at the intersection of creative production and AI-integrated workflows. The roles require candidates with both production pipeline experience (VFX, real-time, spatial computing, experiential) and AI/ML development credibility. Most AI search firms work the AI side without the production depth. Most creative tech recruiters work the production side without the AI leadership capability. FourPointZero covers both.

**What types of roles does CreativAI place?**

Senior permanent roles at the intersection of AI and creative production: CAIO, Head of AI Production, Creative Technology Director, VP AI, Head of ML Engineering in VFX, Executive Producer with AI mandate. The common requirement is that candidates have shipped AI in actual production environments, not just developed AI products.

**Who is this practice for?**

Established companies with a confirmed AI mandate at board or executive level. 50+ employees, budget for a £150K+ senior appointment, active or imminent search underway. We are not the right partner for organisations at the AI strategy stage with no active hiring need.

**How long does a search take?**

Typically 8 to 12 weeks from signed brief to shortlist. That is faster than most organisations achieve through internal TA for this candidate profile, because the network is already built.

**What is the engagement model?**

Retained search. A small engagement fee is payable on commencement, with the balance due on placement. First-time retained clients can access a structured engagement option: a fixed engagement fee with the full success fee payable only on placement.

**Geographic coverage?**

UK and US.

**How do I know if my mandate is in scope?**

A 15-minute call is enough. We will tell you directly whether the mandate fits. No pitch, no commitment.
