## Post

**CreativAI** is FourPointZero's dedicated practice for senior AI leadership in creative production.

For the past eighteen months, the same brief has been landing in my inbox from different companies.

Find someone who can lead AI integration into our production pipeline. Director level. Has to understand Unreal, Nuke, or Houdini under real deadline pressure. Has to be credible with the creative team, not just the tech team.

The same 50 to 75 candidates globally. Every time.

The problem wasn't the talent pool.

AI firms don't know production. VFX firms don't know AI leadership. Most of these briefs were sitting in a gap neither market had properly mapped, and it kept coming up in search after search.

So I built something to sit there.

VFX studios, virtual production, spatial computing, creative SaaS, media owners. Director level and above. Genuine delivery track record on both sides. That's the bar.

We built this because the market needed it. Not because AI is trending.

We've been placing at this intersection since 2019. CreativAI is the name we put on work we were already doing.

If your studio has an AI mandate at board level and a senior search coming, I would welcome a conversation.

P.S. A 15-minute call is enough to tell you whether your role sits within our network. No pitch. No commitment.
