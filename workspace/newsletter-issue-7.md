Earlier this week, we launched CreativAI: FourPointZero's practice for AI leadership placement in creative production.

I want to explain the thinking behind it, rather than just announce it.

---

## The problem we kept seeing

Over the past two years, a particular type of brief started appearing consistently. Not a creative director. Not a pure AI product lead. Something at the boundary: a senior leader who could own AI integration inside a live production environment, hold their own technically, and take the creative team with them.

That brief was, consistently, the hardest one we saw.

The candidates who genuinely have both sides, AI technical depth and creative production credibility at a senior level, are a small group. They are not on job boards. Most are approached regularly by firms that do not understand the role well enough to have a useful conversation. The contact noise is high. The signal is low.

The brief itself was often the first obstacle. Roles drafted by the technology function looked entirely different from roles drafted by the production side. Neither version reliably found the right person. After shortlists stalled, the conversation was always the same: the network exists, but reaching it requires genuine knowledge of both sides of this space.

We had that conversation often enough that it eventually became a practice.

---

## What CreativAI does differently

CreativAI is a formalisation of what we had already been doing. The name is new. The approach is not.

The biggest difference is where the search starts. We challenge the brief before we begin. If the way the role is written will not find the right person, we say so before the search opens. A brief that is right from the start saves months of stalled process and a second search fee.

The candidate network is not a database. These are relationships built over six years through AIXR, VRARA, and direct work in VFX, spatial computing, and virtual production. When we approach a senior candidate, the call lands because the relationship predates the mandate. In a pool this concentrated, that is the only sourcing that works.

Searches run by the practice typically take eight to twelve weeks from signed brief to a shortlist of three to five qualified candidates. Faster than most organisations achieve through internal sourcing for this profile, because we were not starting from scratch.
