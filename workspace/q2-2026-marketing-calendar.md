## CreativAI Post-Launch Content Arc

The four phases below map to the post-17-April content arc. Each phase has a different purpose and calls for a different voice.

### Phase 1 — Launch (17–30 Apr)
**Purpose:** Announce. Explain why. Generate curiosity and credibility simultaneously.
**Tone:** Confident, story-led. Not promotional. Personal.
**Channels:** D-3, D-1, D-0 posts. Newsletter 7. D+3 and D+7 posts.
**Key message:** CreativAI exists because this specific problem kept appearing. We built a practice for it. Here's what that means.

### Phase 2 — Evidence (1 May–15 Jun)
**Purpose:** Show that the practice works. Move from announcement to demonstration.
**Tone:** Observational, authoritative. Share what we're seeing, not what we're claiming.
**Channels:** Newsletter 8 (what the first fortnight told us). May LinkedIn posts. Blog post on post-production pipeline.
**Key message:** The early searches are confirming what we expected — and revealing something we didn't.

### Phase 3 — Case Studies (Jun–Jul)
**Purpose:** Convert interest into conversations. Concrete examples replace positioning statements.
**Tone:** Specific, factual, anonymised where needed. Let outcomes speak.
**Channels:** June blog post. Newsletter 9. LinkedIn post series (to be briefed in late May).
**Key message:** Here's what a CreativAI search looks like from brief to placement.

### Phase 4 — Community (Jul onwards — Q3)
**Purpose:** Build a network around the practice, not just awareness of it. Candidates who know they belong here. Clients who recommend us.
**Tone:** Invitational. Less broadcast, more conversation.
**Channels:** LinkedIn engagement posts. Possibly a candidate-facing newsletter strand. Events presence at Q3 events.
**Key message:** This is where AI creative leaders come when they want a conversation with people who understand the work.
