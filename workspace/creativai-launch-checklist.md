## First 48 Hours Post-Launch (D+0 to D+2)

**Added:** 9 April 2026 — Customer Success (FOU-419)
**Context:** The launch post goes live Thursday 17 April at 9am BST. The window immediately after is where momentum is made or lost. These five actions are sequenced to maximise reach and signal quality — not to look busy.

---

### Action 1 — LinkedIn: First 2 hours after posting (9am–11am BST)

**Respond to every comment within the first hour.**

LinkedIn's algorithm weights early engagement heavily. Comments received in the first 60 minutes determine how broadly the post is distributed beyond your immediate network. Every reply you post counts as fresh engagement and extends that window.

- Reply individually to each comment — no generic "Thanks for the support" responses
- If someone asks a specific question, answer it directly; this generates sub-thread activity
- If a VIP contact comments (particularly Namit, Jody, David), respond within minutes and personalise the reply
- Do not edit the post once it is live — edits reset the engagement clock on some feeds
- Keep your phone nearby for the first hour; notifications matter more than normal today

**Time required:** 60–90 minutes active monitoring.

---

**Two moves. Do both in one sitting.**

Reshare the personal post from the FourPointZero company page within 2–3 hours of the original going live. Do not copy-paste — use LinkedIn's native reshare function. Add one sentence of company-voice commentary, e.g.:

### Action 5 — One thing NOT to do

**Do not post reactively based on vanity metrics in the first 48 hours.**

If the launch post reaches fewer impressions than expected, do not follow it immediately with a second post, a "thread", or a "clarification". The most common mistake in the first 48 hours is diluting the launch signal by flooding your feed.

The algorithm does not reward panic. A second post the same day competes with the first for feed space in your own network's view.

Specific patterns to avoid:
- Do not post a screenshot of the post "in case anyone missed it"
- Do not boost with a paid promotion before you have seen 24 hours of organic data
- Do not repost any engagement statistics publicly (e.g. "1,000 impressions — thank you all!") — this reads as vanity rather than credibility
- Do not edit the caption of the live post to add missed hashtags or links

If momentum is strong by end of D+1, the right response is **a reply to a comment** or **a personal DM to a specific person who engaged** — not a new post. The next scheduled LinkedIn content piece handles D+3 amplification naturally. Let the schedule do its job.
