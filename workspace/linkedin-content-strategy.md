## 1. Positioning Alignment

**One-liner:** FourPointZero is the only executive search practice built from inside creative production, for the leaders navigating AI transformation of how creative work gets made.

**LinkedIn territory:** FPZ owns the intersection of creative talent and AI transformation. Every post should be answerable to: *"Why would a studio director or VFX head stop scrolling for this?"*

**Voice check:** Not a recruiter posting jobs. Not an AI evangelist. A strategic advisor who talks to studios and candidates every day and sees what others don't. Knowledgeable about challenges, curious about solutions, connected to the right people.

**What the profile must signal:** Informed industry observer. Six years of creative production placement depth. A firm that understands the AI transformation of creative workflows from the inside — not from the sidelines.

## 2. Content Pillars

Each pillar tested against: (1) genuine expertise, (2) target audience actively cares, (3) sustainable for 6+ months, (4) connects to what FPZ sells.

### Pillar 1 — Role Evolution at the AI/Creative Intersection (35%)

**What it covers:** How specific creative production roles are being rewritten by AI. Creative Directors, Heads of Production, Head of AI VFX, CAIO, VP AI Production. What the new brief looks like versus the old one. Where most studios are getting it wrong.

**Why it works:** Senior leaders feel this tension every day but rarely see it articulated clearly. FPZ has the network intelligence to name it specifically.

**Goal connection:** Positions FPZ as the firm thinking ahead of the role brief — which is exactly where retained executive search earns its fee.

**Example angles:**
- "The CD role is quietly splitting into two different jobs"
- "The Head of Production brief hasn't changed. The job has."
- "The CAIO title is arriving at creative companies. The hiring infrastructure for it hasn't."

---

### Pillar 2 — Talent Market Intelligence (30%)

**What it covers:** What studios are struggling to find and why. Where the real talent pool is. What candidates in this space actually want (it rarely matches what studios assume). Compensation and career trajectory signals.

**Why it works:** Decision-makers in this space are time-poor and information-hungry. Proprietary market intelligence is a differentiated signal that no job board or generalist recruiter can match.

**Goal connection:** Demonstrates FPZ's candidate access — the core value proposition of retained search. Every post in this pillar is a soft proof point for "we know who's available before anyone else does."

**Example angles:**
- "The candidates studios say don't exist — they do. They're just not looking."
- "What senior AI-creative candidates actually want (it's not always equity)"
- "Where the Tier 1 AI-in-production talent pool is actually based"

---

### Pillar 3 — Leadership at the AI/Creative Intersection (20%)

**What it covers:** What it takes to lead AI transformation in creative production. Competencies that matter at DNEG versus at a creative agency versus at a SaaS platform. How to evaluate candidates for roles that don't yet have a settled definition. Org design questions studios are wrestling with.

**Why it works:** Addresses the hiring decision-maker's practical problem: "I need to fill this role and I'm not sure what I'm looking for." FPZ being able to answer this credibly is the entire business case.

**Goal connection:** Directly accelerates BD conversations. A studio director who reads three posts in this pillar already trusts that FPZ understands the brief.

**Example angles:**
- "How to interview for AI fluency in a creative production role"
- "Writing a job spec for a role that doesn't quite exist yet"
- "Why 'we'll promote internally' is the wrong default for AI leadership at studios"

---

### Pillar 4 — Industry Signals and Adjacent Context (15%)

**What it covers:** News and events from the creative production and AI space, filtered through a talent and leadership lens. SIGGRAPH, VFX Voice, major studio announcements, AI industry developments that touch creative production. Always connected to: "what does this mean for how studios hire and how leaders build careers?"

**Why it works:** Establishes FPZ as an active, connected participant in this world — not a service provider looking in from outside. Timeliness boosts algorithm distribution.

**Goal connection:** Broadens reach and earns profile-post alignment signals from LinkedIn's algorithm. Attracts followers who aren't yet in-market but will be.

**Example angles:**
- "What [studio announcement] means for the next wave of AI production hiring"
- "Spatial computing is quietly creating a new leadership category"
- Reactions to VFX Voice or The Drum pieces — with the FPZ talent-market angle added

## 3. Weekly Posting Rhythm

**Cadence:** 4 posts per week. If content quality is at risk, default to 3 — consistency beats volume at this stage of audience development.

| Day | Content Type | Pillar |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| Monday | Industry intelligence | Pillar 2 or 4 |
| Tuesday | Tactical/how-to | Pillar 3 |
| Thursday | Opinion or contrarian take | Pillar 1 or 3 |
| Friday | Market intelligence or personal story | Pillar 2 or 1 |

**Posting time:** 7:30–8:30 am UK time (catches UK morning commute and US East Coast early afternoon). Thursday can post at 12:00 pm for mid-day reach.

**First 60 minutes:** The algorithm uses early engagement velocity to set distribution. Engage with comments immediately after posting — even brief replies count.

## 4. Format Mix

| Format | Frequency | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Observation post (3–5 short paragraphs, ends with genuine question) | 40% | Core FPZ format — see validation-lgd-linkedin-post.md as voice reference |
| Opinion/contrarian take (punchy, clear stance, specific evidence from market) | 25% | Tier 1 engagement driver per benchmark data |
| Numbered list with bold headers | 15% | Use sparingly — highest save rate but risks looking generic |
| Platform or industry commentary | 10% | Timely hooks only — not forced reactions |
| Social proof / placement signal | 10% | Never announce jobs directly — frame as market intelligence |

**Post length:** 180–270 words. LinkedIn suppresses under 100 and over ~350 words in feed.

**Format rules (non-negotiable):**
- No hashtags
- No emoji (unless specifically requested for a post)
- Max 2 bolded phrases per post
- P.S. line on most posts — soft positioning close, never a hard sell
- Every post in first person. "I spoke with...", "In six years of placing...", "What I'm seeing..."
- Paragraphs: 1–2 sentences maximum
- No hedge words: "suggests", "indicates", "appears to", "seems to"
- No business clichés: "game-changer", "disruptive", "journey", "landscape"

## 7. Engagement Plan

**The rule:** Commenting on other people's content is the single highest-leverage LinkedIn activity at this stage — ahead of posting.

**Daily target:** 5 substantive comments on target accounts and senior figures. One thoughtful comment outperforms ten "great post" replies.

**Comment-to-DM pipeline:** After 3–5 meaningful interactions with an individual, a connection request or DM is natural rather than cold.

**Priority accounts to monitor and engage:**

| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| Key publication accounts | VFX Voice, The Drum, Campaign, Kidscreen, ProVideo Coalition |
| Target studio and software companies | DNEG, Framestore, Foundry, SideFX, Maxon, Adobe Creative Cloud |
| AI-native creative companies | Runway, ElevenLabs, Luma AI |
| Industry bodies | VES, SIGGRAPH, AIXR, VRARA |
| Senior individuals (potential clients) | CAIO/Heads of AI at WPP agency brands, GroupM, FOX Entertainment, BBC Studios |
| Senior individuals (potential candidates) | VFX supervisors and heads of production who are visibly engaging with AI topics |

**Comment style rules:**
- Add a specific perspective — not agreement
- Reference something from FPZ's search experience when relevant (without naming clients)
- Keep to 2–4 sentences
- Ask a follow-on question if appropriate

## 8. Self-Critique Notes

**Cadence risk:** Four posts per week is ambitious for an early-stage content operation. If quality drops, reduce to three. Consistency at lower volume outperforms inconsistency at higher volume for sub-10K audiences.

**Calendar buffer:** The Technical Writer quality gate adds 24–48 hours. All posts should be briefed to TW at least two working days before publish date.

**Evergreen reserve:** Keep 2–3 fully written, TW-cleared posts held back at all times. These absorb calendar slippage without a gap appearing in feed.

**Voice drift risk:** The biggest failure mode for FPZ LinkedIn content is sliding from "active recruiter in market" to "content strategist writing about the market." Every post should feel anchored in something real: a search conversation, a candidate brief, a client's confusion. If a post could have been written by anyone, it should not be published.

**AI detection risk:** LinkedIn's 360Brew evaluates originality. All posts must pass through Technical Writer's de-ai-ify quality gate to protect distribution reach.

**Pillar concentration:** Track which pillar generates the most saves and comments at the end of each week. At the 30-day review, double down on the top performer and reduce the weakest.

## 9. Thirty-Day Review Triggers

Review and adjust strategy when any of the following occur:

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| A post receives 10+ saves | Replicate the format, topic territory, and hook structure immediately |
| A post receives fewer than 5 reactions after 24 hours | Audit: hook strength, post time, pillar alignment, voice drift |
| A pillar is generating 0 comments at end of week 2 | Review angle and format — not the pillar itself |
| Comment-to-DM pipeline produces a BD conversation | Note which post and format triggered it. Replicate |
| End of week 4 | Full review: best-performing pillar, format mix, posting time optimisation |

**30-day goal:** 3 posts with 10+ saves, 1 conversation with a prospective client sourced from LinkedIn engagement, consistent weekly cadence maintained across all 4 weeks.
