## Universal Template — Unexpected Responses

For replies that do not fit YES, NOT NOW, or referral clearly.

> Thanks for coming back to me. [ACKNOWLEDGE WHAT THEY SAID IN ONE SENTENCE — do not repeat it at length.]
>
> [ONE SENTENCE connecting their point back to a relevant observation from your end — keep it factual, no pitch.]
>
> Happy to keep this as an occasional exchange of notes rather than a formal conversation — [COMPANY]'s direction is worth following regardless. Does that work?

**When to use this:** A reply that is harder to classify — a philosophical comment, a challenge, an unexpected interest in a different part of the business. The goal is to stay in the conversation without escalating to a full pitch.
