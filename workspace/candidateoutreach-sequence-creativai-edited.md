# FPZ CreativAI: Candidate Outreach Sequence System

**Owner:** Martyn Makinson, FourPointZero
**Practice:** CreativAI
**Platform:** LinkedIn DM (primary) / Email (follow-up where available)
**Language:** UK English
**Goal:** Open conversations with senior AI-creative leaders. Not pitching roles.
**Sequence:** 3 touches over 21 days
**Quality gate:** Route to Technical Writer for humaniser pass before any messages are sent.
**Source:** Candidate map [workspace/candidate-map.md]

---

## How to Use This System

1. Select the persona group that matches your candidate.
2. Research their specific signals before writing (see Research Protocol below).
3. Replace all `[bracketed placeholders]` with candidate-specific detail.
4. Run each message through the self-critique checklist before sending.
5. Log every send in the Pipeline Tracker at the bottom of this document.
6. Route final messages to Technical Writer for humaniser pass before sending.

**Rule:** Do not send Touch 1 without at least one named signal from research. No generic messages.

## Research Protocol (Run Before Every Outreach)

Gather at least one specific signal before writing any message:

| Signal Type | Strength | Where to Find |
|---|---|---|
| Recent LinkedIn post by the candidate | Strong | LinkedIn search: [Name] posts last 30 days |
| Company AI announcement or press coverage | Strong | Google: [Company] AI 2025 OR 2026 |
| Conference talk or panel (SIGGRAPH, Cannes Lions, SXSW) | Strong | YouTube, conference programmes |
| Published article or interview | Medium | Google: [Name] interview OR article |
| Role change or new appointment | Medium | LinkedIn notifications, trade press |
| Company-level news only | Weak | Use Persona E approach only |

**If no signals found:** Do not send. Add to backlog for research follow-up in 30 days.

## Persona A: CAIO / Inaugural Chief AI Officer

**Profile:** These individuals defined an entirely new function. They have the highest public profile, the most inbound from recruiters, and zero tolerance for generic outreach. Every message must reference something specific they have said, published, or built.

**Approach angle:** Acknowledge what they built. Ask what they learned. Position Martyn as building the definitive picture of AI leadership in creative — not placing, researching.

**Personalisation hooks:**
- LinkedIn thought leadership posts (especially posts that take a position on the AI creative market)
- Conference panels (Cannes Lions, Davos, SXSW, AI Summit)
- Company AI strategy announcements they led or were quoted in
- Published interviews or op-eds

---

### Touch 1 — LinkedIn DM (Day 0)

**Purpose:** Open with a specific, non-pitchy observation. Invite connection, not a call.

```
[Name] — [specific reference: e.g. "your recent post on the difference between AI adoption and AI transformation in creative organisations" / "the CAIO brief you've built at [Company]" / "your panel at Cannes Lions on [topic]"] clarified something I've been mapping across a number of conversations.

I'm building a research picture of how senior AI leadership is actually structured in creative industries — not the job specs, the lived reality. Your perspective on what the mandate actually requires would be useful.

Happy to connect. Martyn Makinson, FourPointZero.
```

**Word count target:** Under 100 words.
**Tone check:** Peer-to-peer, not recruiter-to-candidate.
**Hard rule:** Do not mention roles, opportunities, or placement.

**Example (Daniel Hulme, WPP):**
```
Daniel — your framing of AI as infrastructure rather than capability at the recent WPP Open event reframed something I'd been thinking about differently.

I'm mapping how the CAIO mandate is actually operating inside holding companies vs how it's being described publicly — the gap is significant. Your perspective on what it actually requires would be worth having.

Happy to connect. Martyn Makinson, FourPointZero.
```

---

### Touch 2 — LinkedIn DM (Day 7–10)

**Purpose:** Add genuine value. One market insight or research finding relevant to their situation. Not a follow-up for follow-up's sake.

```
[Name] — sharing something relevant to your work: [specific FPZ market insight, e.g. "we're seeing a bifurcation in how holding companies are structuring AI mandates — CAIO at parent-company level vs embedded at agency-brand level — and what each role actually controls is fundamentally different. The title looks the same from outside."].

Relevant given [their specific company context or something they've discussed publicly]. Happy to share the full picture from the research if useful.

Martyn.
```

**Value add options for this persona:**
- FPZ finding on how inaugural AI roles are being scoped vs reshaped 12 months in
- Observation on where the CAIO mandate is moving into retained search territory
- Relevant industry data point from a recent interview or report (cite it)

**Example (John Kahan, Stagwell):**
```
John — sharing something relevant: we're seeing inaugural CAIO appointments at holding companies bifurcate in year two — some move toward enterprise AI infrastructure ownership, others toward creative capability building. Very different roles, same title.

Given you're 12 months into building the mandate at Stagwell, be interested in which direction you see it pulling. Happy to share what we're seeing across the market.

Martyn.
```

---

### Touch 3 — LinkedIn DM (Day 14–21)

**Purpose:** Light close. Give them an easy, pressure-free way to engage or exit.

```
[Name] — I'll leave this with you.

If you're ever open to an off-the-record conversation about how AI leadership in creative is evolving — not placement, just market perspective — I'd find it genuinely valuable. The research I'm building is better for having voices like yours in it.

Following your work either way. Martyn.
```

**Do not:** Mention roles. Do not say "I have something exciting". Do not follow up again after this touch.

**Post-Touch 3 action:** Add to 6-month re-engagement list. Note the date and any response signals.

**Personalisation hooks:**

## Self-Critique Checklist

Run this before sending any message:

- [ ] Does Touch 1 reference a specific, named signal from research? (Not "I came across your profile")
- [ ] Is Touch 1 under 100 words?
- [ ] Does Touch 1 end without a pitch, a call request, or mention of roles?
- [ ] Does Touch 2 add something genuinely new — not just "following up to see if you saw my last message"?
- [ ] Does Touch 3 give the candidate an easy, pressure-free exit?
- [ ] Has every `[bracketed placeholder]` been replaced with candidate-specific detail?
- [ ] Is the message in UK English throughout?
- [ ] Has the message been routed to Technical Writer for humaniser pass?

If any item is not checked, revise before sending.

## Approach Sequence Recommended for Initial Campaign

Start with Personas C and E. Reasons:
1. Lower public profile than Persona A — less inbound noise, higher response probability
2. More likely to engage on the career trajectory angle
3. Build message refinement from these cohorts before approaching Tier 1 CAIO candidates

**Wave 1 (Week 1–2):** Personas C and E — 6–8 candidates
**Wave 2 (Week 3–4):** Persona B — VFX ML leaders

**Persona D:** As and when specific LinkedIn profiles for BBC/FOX roles are identified
