## 2. Banner Spec

### Format

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Dimensions | 1,128 × 191px (minimum) |
| Design at | 2× (2,256 × 382px) for retina |
| File format | PNG or JPG |
| Text safe area | Centre 60% of width; avoid left and right edges where LinkedIn overlays company logo |

---

### Visual Direction

**Background:** Deep charcoal or near-black. Industry-appropriate; positions FPZ away from the bright-gradient aesthetic common in generalist recruiters. Signals authority, not approachability.

**Typography:** Clean, modern sans-serif (match whatever typeface is in the FPZ/CreativAI brand kit). No decorative or script fonts.

**Layout:** Text left-aligned within the safe zone. Optional: FPZ and CreativAI logo marks in the bottom-right corner at small size.

---

### Copy

**Primary line (large, bold):**
> Executive search for AI in creative production

**Secondary line (smaller, lighter weight, directly below):**
> FourPointZero · CreativAI

No further copy. The banner's only job is to confirm the visitor is in the right place.

---

### What to avoid

- Stock imagery (handshakes, office environments, generic people at screens) — signals mid-market generalist
- Abstract technology imagery (circuit boards, glowing nodes) — overused, adds nothing
- The company name large in the banner — it is already on the page
- Logos only without supporting text — wasted on visitors who don't yet know FPZ

---

### Optional background treatment

A very low-opacity geometric or abstract texture — think a subtle grid or minimal line pattern — can add depth without distraction. Must be clearly secondary to the text. Not required; a clean dark background with the text works well on its own.
