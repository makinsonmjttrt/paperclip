## 2. Company Boilerplate

*(100 words — standard 'About FourPointZero' paragraph for press releases)*

FourPointZero is a specialist executive search firm for creative production and emerging technology. Founded in 2019, the firm places senior leadership talent at studios and agencies working across VFX, virtual production, spatial computing, experiential, and real-time workflows. Its CreativAI practice focuses on executive search at the intersection of AI and creative production, placing the senior operators who run AI-integrated pipelines at major studios and independent agencies across the UK and US. FourPointZero operates on a retained basis for director-level roles and above. The firm is a member of APSCO, VRARA, and AIXR.

## 3. CreativAI Product Description

*(75 words — for product listing sites, directories, press kits)*

CreativAI is FourPointZero's executive search practice for senior leaders where AI meets creative production. It places Heads of Production, Executive Producers, and senior technology leaders at VFX studios, spatial computing companies, and experiential agencies running AI-integrated pipelines. Generalist recruiters can assess AI credentials or production experience — not both. CreativAI shortlists on both. All mandates are director-level and above. The firm has built its candidate network in this sector since 2019.
