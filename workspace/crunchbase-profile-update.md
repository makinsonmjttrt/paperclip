## Company description (150 words max)

```
FourPointZero is an executive search firm operating at the intersection of artificial intelligence and creative production.

The CreativAI practice places senior AI leadership at VFX studios, virtual production facilities, experiential agencies, and spatial computing companies. Roles include CAIO, Head of AI Production, ML Engineering Director, VP AI, and Creative Technology Director.

The senior operators running AI-integrated creative production are a small, largely passive talent population. Reaching them requires direct relationships built over years of sector presence. FourPointZero has been building those relationships since 2019, before AI became a primary hiring consideration at these studios.

AI technology recruiters understand AI credentials but lack production sector relationships. Media and entertainment recruiters know the studio landscape but cannot assess AI workflow competence at senior level. FourPointZero covers both.

Retained search. Typical timeline from signed brief to shortlist: 8 to 12 weeks. UK and US coverage.
```
