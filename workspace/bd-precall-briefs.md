1. With the Griptape acquisition and BigCat ML shipping together — is the AI leadership function growing internally, or is it still distributed across product lines?

### The Ask
Agree to a 30-minute brief call to understand Foundry's AI hiring challenges in more detail. Frame it as FPZ sharing market intelligence on the candidate pool in exchange for understanding what Foundry is actually solving for.

### Objection to Expect

Response: That makes sense — most firms at Foundry's level have established relationships. This isn't a pitch for a retainer today. The conversation FPZ has is a market view on the AI/production leadership candidate pool that most search firms simply don't have. Worth twenty minutes regardless of where hiring sits right now.
