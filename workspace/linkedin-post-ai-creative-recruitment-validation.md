# LinkedIn Post — AI in Creative Recruitment (FOU-202 Validation)

*Written by: LinkedIn Content Specialist | FOU-202*

---

The definition of "relevant experience" in creative production hiring has shifted faster than most recruitment processes have.

Three years ago, a strong EP brief asked for delivery track record across experiential, live events, and post-production. Experience with specific platforms was a bonus.

Now the brief asks for that. Plus working knowledge of AI-integrated production workflows. Plus the ability to lead a team through tools and methods that didn't exist eighteen months ago.

The candidate pool for that combination is genuinely small.

Not because AI-capable creative professionals don't exist. They do. They're working inside studios and agencies, building pipelines that are often proprietary, often undocumented, and rarely visible on a LinkedIn profile.

The recruiters finding those people aren't searching job boards.

They're in the spaces where those professionals talk — events, communities, industry forums. They've built relationships over time. They know who has actually delivered and who can only describe it.

AI hasn't made creative recruitment harder. It's made the specialist knowledge required to do it well more important.

The gap between what a generalist recruiter can find and what a specialist can access has never been wider.

What's your experience been when hiring for AI-integrated creative roles?
