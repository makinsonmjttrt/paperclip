### Message 2 — First Message (After Accept, 24-48 hours)

> Thanks for connecting.

> I run CreativAI, the AI practice at FourPointZero. We place senior leaders in VFX, virtual production, and creative technology — specifically people who can hold the craft side and the AI pipeline side at the same time.

> It's a small candidate pool. Most search firms in this space don't have the VFX background to assess it properly.

> Is AI integration in the production pipeline something you're actively building towards at [Studio], or is it more early-stage right now? Happy to share what I'm seeing at similar studios either way.

*Word count: ~90 | Tone: direct, peer conversation, question invites response without pressure*

---

### Message 3 — Follow-Up (Day 7)

> One more thought since connecting.

> The challenge I keep hearing from VFX studio heads right now is the same: the people who understand generative AI deeply enough to build it into a production pipeline rarely have the production leadership experience to actually run a floor. And the experienced production leaders are still building their AI literacy.

> It's a small overlap. That's the gap we focus on.

> If that's a live conversation at [Studio] — or just one worth having — I'm easy to reach.

> Martyn Makinson, FourPointZero / CreativAI

*Word count: ~86 | Adds new angle (the dual-competency gap) — not a re-send of Message 2*
