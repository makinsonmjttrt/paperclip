## Before You Start

You need **Super Admin** access to the FourPointZero LinkedIn Company Page. If you cannot see the "Edit page" button when you visit the page, you are not logged in as an admin — check you are on the correct LinkedIn account.

## Step 1 — Access the Company Page Admin

1. Go to [linkedin.com](https://www.linkedin.com) and log in.
2. In the top navigation bar, click your profile photo (top right).
3. Select **Manage** under "Company pages" — or go directly to:
   `https://www.linkedin.com/company/fourpointzero/admin/`
4. You will land on the admin dashboard. Click **Edit page** (top right of the page, below the banner).

---

## Step 2 — Update the Tagline

The tagline is the short descriptor that appears just below the company name in search results and on the page header.

1. In Edit page, scroll to the **Tagline** field (near the top, under the company name and logo).
2. Delete whatever is currently there.
3. Paste this exactly:

```

```

4. Click **Save** (bottom of the edit panel).

**Character limit:** 120 characters. This tagline is 50 characters.

## Step 4 — Update the Banner Image

The current banner needs replacing. You have two options:

**Option A — Brief a designer**
Send them this exact brief:

| Spec | Value |
|------|-------|
| Dimensions | 1,128 × 191px (minimum) — design at 2× (2,256 × 382px) for retina |
| File format | PNG or JPG |
| Background | Deep charcoal or near-black |
| Typography | Clean modern sans-serif, matching FPZ/CreativAI brand |
| Text safe area | Centre 60% of width — avoid left and right edges |
| Primary line (large, bold) | Executive search for AI in creative production |
| Secondary line (smaller) | FourPointZero · CreativAI |
| Optional | FPZ and CreativAI logo marks, small, bottom right |
| Avoid | Stock imagery, circuit boards, company name large in banner |

**Option B — Use Canva**
1. Go to [canva.com](https://www.canva.com) and search for "LinkedIn Banner".
2. Select a template at 1,128 × 191px.
3. Set a dark background (near-black or deep charcoal).
4. Add the two lines of text above in a clean sans-serif font.
5. Export as PNG.

**To upload the banner:**
1. In Edit page, hover over the current banner image.
2. Click the **edit (pencil) icon** that appears on the banner.
3. Upload your new image file.
4. Crop/position if prompted, then save.

## Step 5 — URL Slug Change (Optional, 5 minutes admin)

The current Company Page URL contains the old slug `xrjobs`. To update it to `fourpointzero`:

1. In Edit page, go to **Page info**.
2. Look for the **LinkedIn public URL** field.
3. Note: LinkedIn may not allow direct editing here — if so, raise a request via LinkedIn Help Centre: `https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin`
4. Request: change Company Page URL from `/company/xrjobs` to `/company/fourpointzero`.
5. This is an admin-only request and can take a few days for LinkedIn to process.

This step is lower priority than the About and Tagline updates. Do those first.

### Step A — Update Your Headline

LinkedIn shows your headline everywhere your name appears: in search results, connection requests, comment threads, and notifications. It is the highest-impression surface you have.

**Paste this exactly (83 characters):**

```
Founder, FourPointZero & CreativAI | Executive Search for AI in Creative Production
```

**What this does:**
- Names both entities, establishing CreativAI as a live, founded product
- Leads with the function (executive search) rather than a vanity title

**To update:**
