## Brief Overview

Five priority rewrite recommendations, each with rationale and a suggested copy direction. Ordered by expected conversion impact. Items 1-3 are required before any paid or outbound activity drives traffic to the site. Items 4-5 are structural builds with longer lead times.

## Priority 1 — Homepage: Rewrite the Hero Section

**Problem:** The headline "The Recruiters for Creative Production" is generic. It describes the category, not the position. Any XR recruiter could use it. The subheading buries AI in a subordinate clause at the end of a long list. There is no client enquiry CTA above the fold.

**Why this matters first:** Every other channel — LinkedIn, newsletter, referrals, outbound — drives traffic back to the homepage. If the homepage doesn't immediately signal the AI-in-creative-production executive search positioning, first impressions are wasted regardless of how good the referral quality is.

**Suggested copy direction:**

**Headline option A (proposition-led):**
> "The executive search practice for AI leadership in creative production."

**Headline option B (network-led):**
> "We know who's building AI into production pipelines. We place them."

**Headline option C (problem-led):**
> "The role sits at the intersection of two talent markets. Most recruiters can only access one."

**Sub-headline (consistent across all options):**
> "FourPointZero places senior and C-suite leaders at studios, agencies and AI-native companies where production credibility matters as much as AI capability."

**CTAs (two, side by side):**
- Primary: "Start a search" (links to client enquiry page or contact)
- Secondary: "Explore roles" (links to jobs page)

**Social proof bar directly below hero (no scroll required):**
> 300+ senior placements · 89% 18-month retention · 89% repeat clients · Since 2019

**Voice guidance:** Use the shorter, more declarative options (A or B). The audience — senior studio operators and founders — responds to directness. Do not over-explain. Let the positioning breathe.

## Priority 2 — Create a CreativAI Practice Page

**Problem:** The CreativAI practice is the primary growth target for 2026 (30% of FPZ revenue). It has no website presence. There is no page, no URL, no SEO signal, no conversion path for the specific client type who needs AI leadership recruitment.

**Why this matters:** This page should be the destination for:
- LinkedIn posts about the CreativAI practice
- Cold outreach campaigns to AI-native studios and VFX companies
- Inbound organic search for "AI executive search creative production" and related terms
- Any PR or media mention of FPZ's AI work

Without it, all activity in this space drives to a homepage that doesn't confirm the specialisation.

**Recommended URL:** fourpointzero.io/creativai/

**Page structure:**

**1. Hero**
Headline: "Executive search for AI leadership in creative production."
Sub-headline: "CreativAI is FourPointZero's dedicated practice for AI leadership placement — placing the leaders responsible for integrating AI into production pipelines at VFX studios, media owners, creative software companies, and AI-native platforms."

**2. The problem we solve**
Use client language from product-marketing-context.md:
> "The role crosses two talent markets simultaneously. AI/ML technical depth. Creative production credibility. Internal TA can access one. VFX recruiters can access the other. This practice bridges both."

**3. Roles we place**
Short bulleted list:
- Chief AI Officer (CAIO) — inaugural appointments at studios and agency networks
- VP AI Production — at media groups and broadcasters
- Head of ML/AI Engineering — VFX software and studios
- Head of AI Strategy — advertising and brand organisations
- COO / VP Operations — AI-native creative companies

**4. Why FPZ**
Draw from the proof points:
- Six years of placement history at the intersection of creative production and emerging technology
- Proprietary candidate map: the practitioners who have shipped AI in production environments under real deadlines and toolchains
- APSCO, VRARA, AIXR memberships signal sector presence

**5. CTA**
> "If you have a role at this intersection, let's talk."
[Button: Discuss a brief]

**SEO note:** Target these keyword clusters: "AI creative leadership recruitment," "CAIO executive search," "AI VFX talent," "creative production AI hire," "AI pipeline leadership." Include FAQ schema covering: what is CreativAI, what roles do you place, how does the retained search process work.

## Priority 3 — Jobs Page: Separate Executive Search from Mid-Market Listings

**Problem:** The jobs page leads with mid-market technical roles (3D environment artists, Unreal developers at £40-65k). This contradicts the executive search positioning. A client evaluating FPZ for a CAIO hire sees developer roles as the first impression.

**Why this matters:** The fee model (25-33% of first-year base, minimum £24,000) depends on trust that FPZ operates at senior level. The jobs page currently signals the opposite.

**Recommended approach:**

**Option A (preferred): Add an executive search track above the job listings.**
At the top of the Explore Roles page, before any listings, add a content block:

> **Senior and executive search**
> Not all placements are advertised. The most senior and commercially sensitive roles — C-suite, VP, and inaugural AI leadership appointments — are conducted confidentially and matched directly from our candidate network.
> [Button: Discuss a confidential brief]

Then present the public job listings below with a label: "Active listings — senior permanent and contract roles."

**Option B: Create a separate /executive-search/ page** and route the "What We Deliver" navigation item there, keeping /jobs/ for the active listings only.

**Copy direction for the executive search block:**
> "Senior and C-suite hires at this intersection don't go on job boards. We match them from a candidate map built over six years at SIGGRAPH, AWE, and across the networks where this talent operates. If you're working on a confidential brief, start here."

## Priority 4 — About Page: Strengthen the Narrative and Proof Points

**Problem:** The About page has strong statistics but lacks the founding narrative, the specific network advantage, and client voice. The AI proposition is stated observationally rather than as a capability claim.

**Recommended rewrites:**

**Current:** "AI is entering production pipelines alongside established processes, but consistent output is still hard to achieve."

**Suggested direction:**
> "Six years ago, we started placing talent at the edge of creative production — spatial computing, virtual production, real-time. Now AI has moved into every pipeline we work in. We have not pivoted to AI recruitment. We have been recruiting at this intersection since before most firms noticed it existed."

**Statistics block — add context to the numbers:**

| Stat | Current | Suggested addition |
|------|---------|--------------------|
| 300+ roles | bare number | "300+ senior placements since 2019 — 76% at leadership level" |
| 89% retention | bare number | "89% of placed candidates still in role after 18 months — industry benchmark is significantly lower" |
| 89% repeat clients | bare number | "89% of clients return for subsequent hires" |

**Testimonials:** Even one attributed quote from an anonymous client ("Head of Production, major VFX studio") would materially increase trust. Prioritise capturing at least two client quotes and one candidate quote for this page.

## Priority 5 — SEO: Create a Salary and Market Intelligence Page

**Problem:** There is no original data content on the site. The thought leadership gap identified in product-marketing-context.md ("No salary guide or market report exists for this territory. The thought leadership podium is entirely vacant.") is a direct SEO and positioning opportunity.

**Recommended asset:** "AI Creative Production Leadership Salary Guide 2026"

**Rationale:** This single piece would:
- Rank for "AI VFX salary," "creative production AI leadership pay," "CAIO salary UK/US," and related queries — all currently uncontested
- Serve as a lead magnet (gated or ungated) for newsletter sign-ups and research conversation requests
- Position Martyn Makinson and FourPointZero as the only source of salary intelligence at this intersection
- Generate inbound links from industry press (VFX Voice, The Drum, Broadcast, Campaign) if pitched correctly

**Content structure:**
1. Executive summary: key salary movements at the AI-creative production intersection
2. Role-by-role salary ranges: CAIO, VP AI, Head of ML/AI Engineering (by region: UK, US)
3. Market context: demand/supply balance, where the talent is coming from (gaming, tech, academia)
4. FPZ methodology: how we gather data (placement history + network conversations)
5. CTA: "For a confidential view of the market for your specific role, speak to us"

**Page URL:** fourpointzero.io/ai-creative-production-salary-guide-2026/

**Note:** Per product-marketing-context.md guidelines, do not quote specific figures that are not verified against actual placement data. The guide should be built from real placement history. If current data is thin, frame as a "market intelligence brief" and include directional ranges with explicit sourcing.

## Voice Checklist for All Rewrites

Before approving any rewrite:
- [ ] No sentences longer than 20 words unless intentional for rhythm
- [ ] No "disruptive," "synergy," "leverage," "transformational," "revolutionary"
- [ ] No "seeking," "hunting for," or transactional recruiting language
- [ ] "AI experts" avoided — use "informed observer" positioning
- [ ] No unsourced statistics presented as factual
- [ ] UK English throughout
- [ ] Active voice
- [ ] Each page speaks to ONE primary audience (client or candidate — not both)
- [ ] Route final copy through Technical Writer for humaniser quality gate before publishing
