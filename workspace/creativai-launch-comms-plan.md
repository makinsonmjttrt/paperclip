## Overview

This is the day-by-day comms sequence for the CreativAI soft launch. Every item is sequenced against D-0 = 17 April 2026. Where output already exists, the file path is noted. Where new output is needed, the responsible party and brief are included.

The objective of the launch window is not maximum reach — it is conversion quality. The announcement should reach the right 500 people, not 5,000 wrong ones. That means sequencing teaser content before the announcement, warming newsletter subscribers before the page goes live, and following up with proof content after launch to capture late-arriving attention.

### 1. LinkedIn Company Page Bio Update

**Retire this framing:**
Any language that positions CreativAI as coming, building, or in development. Specifically: "building a specialist search practice," "launching in April," "we're developing," or any other future-tense construction about CreativAI's existence.

**Replace with:**

> FourPointZero is a specialist recruitment firm for creative production and emerging technology. Our CreativAI practice places senior leaders at the studios and agencies running AI-integrated production — VFX, experiential, spatial computing, virtual production. We have been building this candidate network since 2019.

**Notes for Martyn:**
- Update the LinkedIn company page About section on April 17, after the page goes live.
- The bio should be in present tense throughout. "We place" not "we will place."
- Keep under 200 characters for the visible preview before the "see more" cut.

**Short-form version (for character-limited preview):**
> Specialist recruitment for AI-integrated creative production. Senior leadership search at VFX studios, experiential agencies, and spatial computing companies.

### 2. Martyn's LinkedIn Headline Update

**Effective from:** April 17 (update same day as launch)

**Current problem:** The headline likely still uses FourPointZero-only framing or pre-CreativAI language. Now that CreativAI is live, Martyn's headline should reflect the specific practice and its market position.

**Recommended headline:**

> Founder, FourPointZero | CreativAI — executive search for AI-integrated creative production

**Alternative (if the above is too long for the field):**

> Founder @ FourPointZero · AI creative production leadership search

**Rationale:**
The headline is Martyn's primary search surface on LinkedIn. Anyone searching for AI creative production, VFX leadership, or spatial computing recruitment should land on this profile. The explicit mention of CreativAI connects the practice to the person. Keep it factual — no taglines, no verbs like "helping" or "passionate about."

---

### 3. NAB 2026 Talking Points — April 19–22

CreativAI launches on April 17. NAB opens on April 19. The two-day gap is an advantage: Martyn can enter every NAB conversation with a concrete fact rather than a teaser.

**The shift in framing:**

Old: "We're building something at the intersection of AI and creative production recruitment."
New: "We launched CreativAI on Friday — our dedicated practice for AI leadership search in VFX and experiential production."

**Core talking points for NAB:**

1. **The launch fact.**
   "We launched CreativAI two days ago — it's our dedicated practice for placing the senior operators running AI-integrated production. VFX, experiential, spatial computing."

2. **The problem it solves.**
   "The issue we kept seeing: generalist AI recruiters assess the AI credentials and miss the production delivery side. Production recruiters know the studio but can't evaluate AI workflow fluency. Both produce shortlists that fail at the third interview. We shortlist on both sides."

3. **The candidate network.**
   "We've been building this candidate network since 2019 — before AI was a hiring consideration at these studios. The senior AI-in-production talent at the major houses know us. That's not replicable with a LinkedIn Recruiter licence."

4. **The soundbite (use in conversation, not on slides).**
   "AI recruiters know the technology. Production recruiters know the studio. We know both sides."

**At NAB specifically:**
- Blackmagic, Epic, Unity, and Adobe all have active or anticipated AI production mandates. If you're in proximity to any of them, the CreativAI launch is the most natural opener you'll have.
- For Blackmagic: the BD Batch 3 brief recommends holding until after NAB — use any signal from the show floor (new AI product, partnership, leadership announcement) as the hook when you send.
- For anyone who asks for the URL: `fourpointzero.io/creativai`

**What NOT to say at NAB:**
Avoid framing CreativAI as a "new thing we're trying." It is a practice built on six years of sector relationships. It is live, it is active, and the candidate network is already in place.
