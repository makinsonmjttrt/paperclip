## Budget Decision Needed: SIGGRAPH + DigiPro LA Trip

The Q2 events plan flagged this as "decision needed by end of May." LA in late July (SIGGRAPH + DigiPro) represents a ~£2,500–3,500 commitment per person. The case:

**For:** SIGGRAPH is the global credibility event for VFX and creative tech. One LA trip catches both DigiPro (technical production practitioners) and SIGGRAPH (studio heads, supervisors, researchers). International profile-building that no European event matches.

**Against:** High cost, US time zone, no confirmed speaking slot. Relationship ROI from a single trip without a panel presence is harder to measure.

**Recommendation:** Go if one of the following applies — (a) a SIGGRAPH contact list of 10+ warm studio targets can be built from the FPZ network, or (b) a DigiPro or SIGGRAPH Birds of a Feather / panel slot is available. Otherwise defer to 2027.

### 1. Develop:Brighton 2026
**Dates:** 14–16 July 2026
**Location:** DoubleTree by Hilton Brighton Metropole, Brighton, UK
**Tier:** B
**FPZ Relevance:** Medium (★★★☆☆)
**Travel:** Day trip or 1 night; ~£200–400 total
**Format:** In-person conference, workshops, networking, Develop:Star Awards (15 July)

**Why attend:** Develop:Brighton is the UK's flagship game development conference, but its relevance to FPZ extends beyond games. The real-time engine professionals here — Unreal and Unity generalists, technical artists, rendering engineers, XR developers — are increasingly the same talent pool feeding virtual production and spatial computing roles. As LED volume and real-time pipelines become standard in film and TV, the crossover between game development and creative production has become direct.

At ~2,000 attendees, Develop is small enough for genuine networking. Low travel cost (Brighton is 60 minutes from London) removes the financial barrier.

**Networking target profile:**
- Technical artists and rendering engineers transitioning to virtual production
- Unreal/Unity generalists working in VFX-adjacent roles
- XR developers and spatial computing leads at game studios
- Head of technology and studio operations at UK mid-size game studios

**FPZ/CreativAI visibility:**
- Speaker submission for 2026 will have closed by April. Target 2027 — a talk on "real-time talent in virtual production" positions FPZ at the intersection of both communities.
- Use the Develop:Star Awards evening (15 July) for informal introductions — this is where studio leads congregate.

**Preparation notes:**
1. Register at developconference.com before end of May.
2. Review the 2026 programme for sessions on AI tools, real-time pipelines, and technical art — these are the rooms where relevant talent concentrates.
3. Identify 6–8 UK game studios with crossover VP or XR work and flag any known contacts attending.
4. Submit a speaker proposal for Develop:Brighton 2027 in Q1 2027 (submission window typically opens September).

### 2. DigiPro 2026 (Digital Production Symposium)
**Dates:** 18 July 2026 (Saturday, day before SIGGRAPH)
**Location:** Los Angeles, USA (co-located with SIGGRAPH)
**Tier:** B+
**FPZ Relevance:** High (★★★★☆) — if LA trip proceeds
**Format:** One-day technical symposium; single track; ~300–500 specialists
**Travel:** Combined with SIGGRAPH — no additional travel cost

**Why attend:** DigiPro is the most specialist event on this calendar. Attendance is small, the audience is exclusively senior production technologists — pipeline TDs, VFX researchers, virtual production leads, AI-in-production specialists. The people presenting at DigiPro are writing the papers that define the next generation of production pipelines. This is the highest-quality room FPZ can walk into.

As CreativAI positions itself as the recruiter who understands production technology, DigiPro is where that claim is stress-tested. Martyn's presence here signals genuine industry engagement, not a generalist firm adding "AI" to its website.

**Networking target profile:**
- Pipeline TDs and VFX R&D leads at major studios
- Production technology directors at ILM, Weta, Framestore, Sony Imageworks, DNEG
- AI and machine learning engineers working on creative production applications
- Virtual production supervisors with technical leadership scope

**FPZ/CreativAI visibility:**
- DigiPro speaker submissions are highly competitive and require a research or production technology contribution. Not realistic for 2026. Attend as a knowledgeable delegate, not a speaker.
- Prepare a clear position on one technical topic (e.g., AI pipeline integration roles, talent structuring for ML-heavy production teams) so conversations have substance.

**Preparation notes:**
1. Only relevant if SIGGRAPH trip is confirmed — DigiPro registration is separate from SIGGRAPH.
2. Check digiproconf.org for 2026 registration dates (typically opens alongside SIGGRAPH).
3. Prepare a short personal narrative explaining FPZ's position in the AI production talent space — DigiPro attendees are technically literate and will probe vague claims.

### 3. SIGGRAPH 2026
**Dates:** 19–23 July 2026
**Location:** Los Angeles Convention Center, Los Angeles, USA
**Tier:** B
**FPZ Relevance:** High (★★★★☆)
**Travel:** Flights + 4–5 nights LA; directionally £1,800–2,500 per person; total with DigiPro ~£2,500–3,500
**Format:** In-person conference, technical sessions, production sessions, Emerging Technologies, exhibition

**Why attend:** SIGGRAPH is the global credibility event for computer graphics, VFX, and creative technology. Attending places FPZ alongside the world's most senior production professionals in a context where the AI-in-production conversation is technically serious. The Production Sessions track is where VFX supervisors and leads present their workflow innovations — exactly the candidate profile CreativAI is serving.

The credibility value of "we were at SIGGRAPH" is real for FPZ's positioning, particularly with US studios expanding European production hubs who may not have encountered FPZ before.

**Networking target profile:**
- VFX supervisors and technical directors at major US and international studios
- Studio heads and production technology leaders (ILM, Weta Digital, Sony Imageworks, Framestore, DNEG, MPC, Industrial Light & Magic)
- AI and machine learning researchers working on creative production applications
- Animation directors and leads integrating AI into character pipeline

**FPZ/CreativAI visibility:**
- SIGGRAPH 2026 speaker submissions closed in late 2025. Attend as a delegate this cycle.
- Target the Production Sessions and Birds of a Feather events for informal introductions.
- Consider hosting a small private dinner (6–10 people) with senior contacts on the evening of 20 or 21 July — this is common practice for agencies and vendors at SIGGRAPH and generates more value than open floor networking.
- Submit a course or production session proposal for SIGGRAPH 2027 in Q4 2026 (deadline typically late November/December).

**Preparation notes:**
1. Decision required by end of May — see Budget Decision section above.
2. Register at s2026.siggraph.org. Full conference pass recommended over exhibits-only.
3. Build a contact list of 10–15 warm studio targets before booking. If that list doesn't exist, reconsider the trip.
4. Book accommodation in DTLA or Mid-Wilshire early — the area fills around SIGGRAPH week.
5. Coordinate with DigiPro — register separately at digiproconf.org. Fly in Friday 17 July to allow setup day.

### 4. Gamescom 2026
**Dates:** 26–30 August 2026
**Location:** Koelnmesse, Cologne, Germany
**Tier:** C (monitor)
**FPZ Relevance:** Low (★★☆☆☆)
**Format:** In-person trade show, 300,000+ consumer and trade attendees

**Recommendation: Skip**

Gamescom is Europe's largest games event, but its audience profile is predominantly consumer-facing publishers, mid-level game developers, and press. The senior creative technology leadership FPZ serves is present in smaller numbers and harder to reach in a crowd of 300,000.

The 2026 programme (August 26–30) falls in a quiet holiday period and involves Cologne travel with no clear IBC synergy (IBC is two weeks later in Amsterdam). The cost-to-value ratio is unfavourable compared to the confirmed Q3 programme.

**Exception:** If FPZ is pursuing a specific relationship with a major game studio entering virtual production (e.g., Ubisoft, Riot, Epic Games), a targeted Gamescom trip with a pre-arranged meeting programme could be justified. Otherwise monitor from afar.

### 5. IBC 2026
**Dates:** 11–14 September 2026
**Location:** Amsterdam RAI Exhibition & Congress Centre, Netherlands
**Tier:** A
**FPZ Relevance:** Very high (★★★★★)
**Travel:** Flights + 3 nights Amsterdam; directionally £900–1,400 per person
**Format:** In-person trade show and conference; 43,000+ attendees from 170+ countries

**Why attend:** IBC is the most important European media technology event on the calendar. For FPZ's purposes, it is where the studio heads, production technology buyers, and senior operators who are actively building AI-augmented production teams gather in one place. The 2026 edition will feature AI-integrated workflows, virtual production, and streaming production technology as centrepiece themes.

Unlike SIGGRAPH (academic and practitioner community) or FMX (artistic and creative), IBC is where purchasing decisions are made and supplier relationships formed. The client profile — broadcasters, production companies, streaming platforms, post houses — maps directly onto CreativAI's hiring client base.

**Networking target profile:**
- Heads of production technology and CTOs at major broadcasters (BBC Studios, Sky, ITV Studios, Channel 4)
- VP and post-production heads at streaming-facing studios (Warner Bros, Netflix production partners, Amazon Studios post)
- Technology and innovation directors at production companies building AI workflows
- Senior engineers and department heads at post houses implementing AI tools

**FPZ/CreativAI visibility:**
- IBC Call for Papers for conference sessions typically closes April/May — check show.ibc.org immediately. A submission on "staffing the AI production team" or "talent gaps in the AI-augmented post-production workflow" could land a panel slot.
- Hosting a fringe dinner or client roundtable on the evening of 11 or 12 September is the single highest-value play at IBC. Target 10–14 senior production technology leaders. A well-curated dinner at a central Amsterdam restaurant builds more pipeline than three days on the show floor.
- Consider the IBC Accelerator programme as a lower-cost sponsorship play to gain official presence without full exhibitor costs.

**Preparation notes:**
1. Register early at show.ibc.org — badge pricing increases as the date approaches. Register in June.
2. **IBC Call for Papers:** Check status immediately. Deadline may still be open. This is a priority action for April/May.
3. Book accommodation in central Amsterdam or near the RAI early — IBC week is fully booked by June.
4. Begin building the fringe dinner guest list in June using Q2 relationship touchpoints from FMX, MPTS, and the AI Summit.
5. Review the IBC Accelerator programme for any relevant sponsorship or participation opportunity.
6. Coordinate Martyn's schedule for all 4 days — split between show floor networking, conference sessions, and the fringe programme.
