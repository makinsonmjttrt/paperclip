Every time I see a studio announce a new AI production initiative, the person leading it is someone I placed two or three years ago.

Not with an AI brief. With a production brief.

They came in as Head of Production, or Technical Supervisor, or Pipeline Lead. The AI mandate arrived later, sometimes much later, because they were already the person in the building who understood both sides of what was changing.

That pattern has repeated often enough that it stopped feeling like coincidence about eighteen months ago.

There is a particular profile of senior creative professional who was always going to end up running AI production. Technically grounded without being tech-first. Delivered under real production pressure. Understands why a creative director will reject a technically perfect output, and can explain AI constraints to someone who thinks in story rather than system architecture.

These people exist. The challenge is that when studios go looking for them, they are using the wrong brief, searching in the wrong places, and competing against tech companies who noticed the same thing two years earlier.

CreativAI is FourPointZero's practice for that specific search.

We've been placing the people who end up running AI production for years. Now we've built a practice around it.

If you're trying to find that profile, or you're that profile, the conversation starts here.
