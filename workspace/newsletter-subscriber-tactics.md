### Tactic 8 — LinkedIn Newsletter Parallel Channel

**What:** Republish Signal as a LinkedIn Newsletter using LinkedIn's native newsletter feature.

**Effort:** 30-minute setup plus 10 minutes per issue to republish.

**How it works:** LinkedIn Newsletters are a separate feature from regular posts. Subscribers receive a notification within LinkedIn when a new issue drops. LinkedIn also prompts your connections to subscribe when you publish. It is a parallel distribution channel that does not replace Beehiiv — it extends reach to the LinkedIn-native audience who won't click out to an external platform.

**Setup:** From Martyn's LinkedIn profile, create a Newsletter titled "Signal" with the same description. Republish each issue as a LinkedIn Newsletter post alongside the Beehiiv version.

**Why this works:** LinkedIn has the Signal audience already. The LinkedIn Newsletter feature reduces friction for people who prefer to consume within the platform. It also contributes to Martyn's creator profile on LinkedIn.

**Expected outcome:** 10–30 LinkedIn Newsletter subscribers from first issue, compounding with follower growth. Different audience segment from Beehiiv — do not treat them as equivalent.

### Tactic 9 — Post-Issue Social Share with Subscribe CTA

**What:** After each Signal issue, publish a LinkedIn post summarising one key insight with a subscribe link.

**Effort:** Agent-produced, Martyn approves and posts.

**Format:**
- Take the strongest insight or argument from the issue
- Write it as a standalone LinkedIn post (not a summary — give the actual insight, which makes the newsletter more credible)
- Close with: "This is from Signal, our weekly briefing on AI creative hiring. If you work in this space: INSERT_BEEHIIV_LINK"

**Why this works:** Each issue becomes two distribution events: the email to existing subscribers, and a LinkedIn post to attract new ones. The post demonstrates the quality of the content rather than pitching it.

**Agent action:** The Content Producer or LinkedIn Growth Director can produce these posts from each issue. Route through humaniser before posting.

**Expected outcome:** 2–5 subscribers per issue from LinkedIn post impressions. Compounds with audience growth.
