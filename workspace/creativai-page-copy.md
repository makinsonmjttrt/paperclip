## 2. The hiring challenge

Senior AI leadership roles in creative production draw from two separate talent markets at once: AI/ML technical depth and creative production credibility. Generalist search firms reach one or the other. Every shortlist fails on one dimension, the role stays open for months, and the programme stalls.

---

## 3. What we do

CreativAI is FourPointZero's specialist executive search practice for AI leadership in creative production. We place CAIOs, Heads of AI Production, Creative Technology Directors, and senior ML engineering leads. We assess candidates on AI delivered under real production constraints: the tools they know, the pipelines they have shipped, the decisions they have owned. We challenge the brief before we start, and we will not take a mandate we do not think we can place.

---

## 4. Who we work with

We work with organisations that have a confirmed AI mandate at board or executive level and an active or imminent senior search.

**Qualifying bar:** established company, 50+ employees, director level and above, £150K+ total compensation.

The four sectors we know best:

**VFX and virtual production studios.** Established studios with confirmed AI transformation programmes. Roles typically sit at the boundary of production pipeline ownership and AI capability delivery.

**Experiential and spatial computing agencies.** Studios building AI-integrated immersive and spatial computing work where candidates need both creative production depth and AI tooling fluency.

**Creative SaaS platforms.** Software vendors with active AI product development mandates needing dedicated senior AI product leadership.

**Media owners and broadcasters.** VP-level mandates, often with confidentiality requirements, for organisations building AI creative labs and production technology programmes.

---

## 5. Why CreativAI

**Network depth, not database breadth.**
We have been placing senior creative production talent since 2019. The Tier 1 AI-in-production candidates at ILM, DNEG, Framestore, Adobe, and Foundry know us. When we approach them, they take the call. We do not scrape LinkedIn.

**Sole focus.**
Every search we run is at the AI/creative production intersection. No other firm is doing this systematically. That focus means our picture of the candidate market is more current and more detailed than any generalist firm can build reactively.

**Dual literacy.**
We assess candidates on AI delivered under real production constraints: Nuke, Houdini, Unreal Engine, ComfyUI under actual deadline pressure. Title and credentials are not a reliable proxy for this. Our shortlists hold up on the technical side and the production side.

---

## 6. How it works

**45-minute brief call.**
We establish the role scope, candidate criteria, and any confidentiality requirements. If the brief will not find the right person, we say so before the search begins.

**Network-only sourcing.**
No job boards. No volume outreach. We approach candidates directly from within our established network.

**Shortlist in 8 to 12 weeks.**
Three to five qualified candidates. We manage the interview process and support offer negotiation.

For clients new to retained search: a small engagement fee on commencement, full success fee on placement.
