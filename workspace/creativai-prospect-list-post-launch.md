**Estimated headcount:** ~330-340
**LinkedIn:** [linkedin.com/company/blue-zoo-animation-studio](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/blue-zoo-animation-studio)

## Outreach Framing Notes

**For UK independents (Blue Zoo, Milk, One of Us, BlueBolt, Territory):**
Lead with the AI talent supply gap — the senior profile that bridges AI tooling and production management is the hardest hire in creative tech right now. FPZ's CreativAI practice exists precisely to find it. The pitch is not "we have CVs" — it is "we know who these people are."

**For studios with active AI investment (Blue Zoo, One of Us, Outpost, BlueBolt):**
Mirror the signal back. Blue Zoo published an AI policy; lead with "you've committed to governing AI — who owns delivery?" One of Us hired a CTO for emerging tech; lead with "what's the next hire he needs?" This shows you've done the work.

**For growth-phase studios (Outpost, Untold, Nexus):**
The leadership need emerges at scale. A 200-person studio operating in four countries has a different talent problem than a 60-person studio in one city. Lead with that — "as you scale, the generalist recruiter problem gets worse, not better."

**For Ghost VFX:**
Relationship-building frame only. No direct hiring pitch on first contact. Lead with industry positioning and creative production AI landscape. Build before pitching.
