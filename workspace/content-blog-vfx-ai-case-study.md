# Why We Built CreativAI

I've been placing senior creative production talent since 2019. For most of that time, AI was a conversation that happened at the edges of the sector. Interesting to some studio heads, theoretical to most.

That changed. Not gradually, not in stages. It changed all at once, somewhere around 2023, when AI stopped being something studios were watching and started being something they were running.

The briefs coming into FourPointZero shifted. Clients who used to ask for Executive Producers with real-time or spatial computing experience started asking for something harder to define: someone who understood those environments and understood AI, specifically understood how AI was changing production at the delivery level. Not AI in the abstract. AI under pipeline pressure.

We had been building relationships with exactly this cohort since before the market knew it needed them. That was coincidence, not foresight. It's just where our network had grown. VFX, virtual production, immersive, spatial computing — the people doing serious technical work in those spaces had already started incorporating AI tools into their practice years before studios were hiring for it. We knew them. We'd placed some of them. We'd stayed in touch.

So when the enquiries accelerated, we could answer them. But the process didn't scale.

## The Problem With Manual Search

Matching a candidate to an AI leadership brief requires understanding both sides of a specific profile: production delivery and AI workflow fluency. These are not the same skills and they don't reliably co-exist. The senior candidate pool for these roles is not large. We knew most of them. What we didn't have was a systematic way to map who was credible against specific production contexts.

Matching from memory and relationship history worked when volume was manageable. By 2024, it wasn't. We were taking more enquiries than a manual process could serve accurately, and the cost of a wrong introduction in a sector this small is high. Everyone knows everyone.

We had two options: hire more people, or build something that made the existing team more accurate.

## The Decision

We built something.

CreativAI is the structured capability layer we put around the candidate network FourPointZero had already built. It's not a job board. It doesn't aggregate CVs. What it does is codify the evaluation criteria that had previously lived in my head and in the experience of our team: what it means to have shipped AI in production, what production contexts map to which AI competencies, which parts of our network have genuine depth versus surface familiarity with a given toolset.

The intent was to make the briefing process more systematic and the matching process faster, without losing the quality signal that comes from knowing people over time.

## What Surprised Us

The part that took longest wasn't the evaluation framework. It was translating production context into a format the matching process could use.

A brief that says "AI leadership role at a VFX studio" describes hundreds of different actual jobs. What the studio does, what the pipeline looks like, what level of AI integration they're at, what the team structure is — all of that changes who the right candidate is. Getting that specificity right before the search starts, not three weeks in, is now built into how we take a brief. That conversation, early, turns out to matter more than anything else in the process.

The other thing: the candidates who matter most in this space are not actively looking and are contacted constantly by recruiters who haven't done the groundwork. Getting them to take the call depends on context and relationship, not message volume. No amount of automation changes that.

## What It Means for Clients

For clients, the difference shows up in shortlist quality and timeline. When someone comes to us with an AI leadership mandate, we're not starting from scratch. We know who in the network is credible against the specific production context, and we know who will take the call. Typical search timeline is 8 to 12 weeks from signed brief to shortlist of three to five candidates. All of them have shipped AI in actual production environments.

For candidates, it means being approached by someone who has read their background properly, not just matched on title.

CreativAI isn't a platform product. It's how FourPointZero does AI executive search. The asset is the network. What changed is the discipline around how we use it.
