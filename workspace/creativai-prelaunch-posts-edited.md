## Post 1 — Monday 14 April (D-3)

**Word count:** ~168
**Format:** Problem post — 'here's what we're solving'
**Hook type:** Bold Personal Declaration
**Structure:** Problem-Agitate-Solution

---

The person most studios are looking for doesn't exist yet.

Not because the talent isn't there.

Because no one has described the role clearly enough to find them.

I've had versions of the same conversation for the last two years.

A studio has decided to lead with AI. The board has backed it. HR has written a brief. Three months later, they're still looking.

The brief is the problem.

When a role sits at the intersection of creative production and AI, neither side of it maps cleanly to what most search firms know how to look for.

𝗔𝗜 specialists often don't have production floor credibility.

𝗖𝗿𝗲𝗮𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 production leaders often haven't worked close enough to the tooling.

The person who has both is rare. Spotting them requires knowing what both sides actually look like from the inside.

Generalist search firms have reach.

They don't have that lens.

Internal hiring surfaces people who are already visible.

These candidates usually aren't.

That's the gap.

That's what we built CreativAI to close.

## Post 2 — Wednesday 16 April (D-1)

**Word count:** ~163
**Format:** Personal 'why' post — quiet confidence
**Hook type:** Time-Stamped Origin Story
**Structure:** Personal narrative, no CTA (intentional — launch is tomorrow)

---

I've been placing creative tech talent since 2019.

Long enough to watch the same search go wrong in the same ways.

A studio builds an AI brief from scratch with nothing to reference. Months pass. Candidates come in technically credible but unable to bring a creative team with them. Or the opposite — respected internally, but the tooling knowledge doesn't hold up. Somewhere between those two wrong answers is the person they actually need.

I didn't build FourPointZero to solve that problem specifically.

But by 2023, it was the only problem anyone was calling me about.

The skills gap at the intersection of AI and creative production isn't a trend. It's a structural feature of how these two worlds developed in parallel without much crossing over. The people who bridged them early are rare, in demand, and almost never on the open market.

CreativAI exists because I've spent six years building the network to reach them.

That's the whole thing.

That's why.
