## Candidate Entry Template

Use this format for each new profile added via LinkedIn Recruiter:

```
| [#] | [Full Name] | [Current Title] | [Company] | [City, Country] | [T1/T2/Adj] | [LinkedIn URL] | [Key notes: background, AI-production credibility signals, approach angle] |
```

**What qualifies as Tier 1:**
- Has shipped AI capability under real creative production constraints (not research/lab, not pure enterprise AI)
- Current or most recent role is at a VFX studio, creative agency with AI mandate, media owner, or creative SaaS
- Has demonstrated ability to bridge AI/ML technical depth with creative production understanding

**What qualifies as Tier 2:**
- Strong AI/ML background but no creative production experience (needs 12 months to build context), OR
- Strong creative production background with emerging AI/ML capability

**Red flags to note:**
- "AI" in title but no creative production or creative tech context
- Pure research/academic AI with no industry delivery record
- Title inflation — "AI Lead" at a 5-person agency is not Tier 2
