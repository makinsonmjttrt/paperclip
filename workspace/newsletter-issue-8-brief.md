### Section 2: The brief problem is worse than we thought

Working title: *"Why role definition is still the first obstacle"*

In the run-up to launch and in early conversations, the same pattern keeps appearing: organisations know they need AI leadership in creative production, but cannot agree internally on what the role is. The creative function writes one brief. The technology function writes another. Neither finds the right person.

This section stays topical regardless of actual BD outcomes. Frame it as a structural observation the launch conversations confirmed — not a new discovery, but something the launch made easier to name clearly.
