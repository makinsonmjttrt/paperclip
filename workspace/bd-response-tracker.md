## Response Protocols

---

### Positive Reply Protocol

They've shown genuine interest — asked a question, requested more information, or indicated openness to a call.

**Do this immediately:**
1. Reply within 24 hours. Do not overthink it.
2. Propose a specific 20-minute slot (two options works better than an open diary link).
3. Keep it brief — you have their attention, don't lose it with a long message.
4. Update tracker: mark **MTG** if a call is confirmed, **POS** if still in dialogue.

**LinkedIn/DM reply template:**

> Thanks — really glad this resonated.
>
> I'd suggest a quick 20-minute call to compare notes. I'm available [DAY at TIME UK] or [DAY at TIME UK] — does either work for you?
>
> Happy to use Zoom, Teams, or a phone call — whatever is easiest.

**Email reply template:**

Subject: Re: [original subject line]

> [FIRST NAME] — great to hear from you.
>
> Happy to set something up. I'm available [DAY at TIME] or [DAY at TIME] — whichever is easier. A 20-minute call is all we need to start.
>
> Martyn Makinson, FourPointZero

**After the call is confirmed:**
- Add a calendar entry.
- Prepare a one-page brief on the company before the call (use `bd-pipeline.md` company context).
- On the call: ask about their actual hiring challenge, not what you can sell them. Listen first.
- Update tracker to **MTG** with the call date in the Notes column.

---

### Soft Positive Protocol

They've acknowledged you — polite, warm even — but haven't committed to anything. Common variants:
- "Not the right moment, but do keep in touch."
- "We're not hiring right now."
- "Interesting — let me have a think."

**Do not chase.** A soft positive handled well becomes a warm lead in 3–6 months.

**Do this:**
1. Reply within 48 hours.
2. Acknowledge what they said. Don't push for a call.
3. Ask one low-friction question or make one specific offer to stay useful.
4. Set a reminder to resurface in 6 weeks.
5. Update tracker: mark **SOFT**. Note the follow-up date.

**LinkedIn/DM reply template:**

> Completely understand — timing is everything in this.
>
> I'll keep an eye on what's developing in your space and drop you a note if anything specifically relevant to [COMPANY] comes across my desk. Is it useful to stay connected for when timing shifts?

**Email reply template:**

Subject: Re: [original subject line]

> [FIRST NAME] — understood, and appreciated.
>
> I'll make a note to circle back in [TIMEFRAME they indicated, or 6 weeks if unspecified]. In the meantime, I'll be building the candidate map in your space regardless — if anything worth sharing comes up, I'll flag it.
>
> Martyn Makinson, FourPointZero

**6-week follow-up (add to your calendar):**

> [FIRST NAME] — following up as promised.
>
> A few things have shifted in the [VFX/creative SaaS/broadcast] AI talent market since we last spoke. Is a quick call more relevant now than it was in [MONTH]?

### Negative / Close Protocol

They've said no. Respect it. A graceful close leaves the door open.

**Do this:**
1. Reply within 24 hours. One message only — do not argue or re-pitch.
2. Thank them. Acknowledge their position.
3. Leave the door open briefly without being desperate.
4. Update tracker: mark **NEG**. Do not contact again unless a significant external signal changes the context (e.g., a major hire or press announcement at their company).

**LinkedIn/DM reply template:**

> Understood — appreciate you taking the time to reply at all. Good luck with whatever's next. I'll follow [COMPANY]'s work regardless.

**Email reply template:**

Subject: Re: [original subject line]

> [FIRST NAME] — completely understood. Appreciate you letting me know.
>
> If things change on the AI leadership front, I'd welcome the conversation at any point. In the meantime, I'll leave you to it.
>
> Martyn Makinson, FourPointZero

## Conversion Milestones

What counts as a win at each stage. Use these to measure progress — not just activity.

| Stage | What it means | What counts as done |
|-------|--------------|---------------------|
| **Connection accepted** | The door is open | LinkedIn connection accepted. No reply required. This is a signal, not a result. |
| **Reply received** | The conversation has started | Any reply — including soft positives and soft nos. A reply means they've acknowledged you exist. Log the response type and act accordingly. |
| **Call booked** | You're in a real conversation | Calendar invite confirmed, both parties. "Let's chat sometime" does not count. A specific date and time counts. |
| **Brief received** | They have a live need | A description of a role or mandate — written or verbal — that is specific enough to write a search brief from. This is the commercial milestone. Everything before this is pipeline development. |

**What a soft no is worth:** A contact who replies "not right now" has acknowledged FourPointZero as a credible outreach. That is not a failure — it is a warm contact. Log it as **SOFT**, set a 6–12 week re-engagement reminder, and treat them as a relationship in progress.

**What the break-up achieves:** Most break-ups don't generate a reply. That's expected. The value is positioning: it signals that FourPointZero doesn't chase, which is the impression you want senior leaders to carry when they eventually do have a need.
