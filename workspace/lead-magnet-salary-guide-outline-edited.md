## Proposed Title

**Primary:** Salary and Hiring Guide for AI Creative Executives: UK 2026

**Sub-title:** What studios and agencies are paying, and how long it really takes to hire, at the intersection of creative production and AI.

---

## Purpose and Positioning

This guide fills a documented gap: no UK recruitment firm has published a salary or hiring benchmark specifically for the AI creative executive layer. FPZ owns this vertical. Publishing a credible, data-backed guide positions FPZ as the category authority and generates qualified leads from hiring decision-makers who are actively planning senior hires in this space.

## Distribution Strategy

### Gating

- Gated via email capture on a dedicated landing page (CreativAI page or fourpointzero.io/salary-guide)
- Form fields: name, company, role, email — minimal friction
- Delivered as PDF via automated email immediately on submission

### Promotion

| Channel | Format |
|---------|--------|
| LinkedIn (personal) | 3-part pre-launch teaser series + launch post with lead form |
| LinkedIn (company page) | Mirror of personal posts |
| CreativAI page | Banner or CTA block linking to guide landing page |
| Newsletter | Dedicated issue announcing launch; link in footer of subsequent issues |
| BD outreach | Reference in follow-up DMs and discovery call prep ("we've just published...") |

### BD Use

- Send guide to warm prospects as a useful reference, no pitch attached
- Reference in BD sequences: "Given you're hiring in this space, this might be useful..."
- Use as a reason to re-engage cold leads who went quiet
