## Good vs Concerning — D-0 Only

These are directional benchmarks, not hard targets. D-0 is a signal check, not a verdict.

| Metric | Looks good | Worth noting |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| LinkedIn impressions by 12pm | Any early traction (post showing 200+ impressions) | Still 0 impressions at 12pm — see playbook |
| LinkedIn comments by 3pm | Any comment from a studio, agency, or creative SaaS contact | Zero comments all day |
| Vercel page views by 6pm | Any visits at all — page is new | Zero views (check the page is live and link works) |
| BD connections accepted | 1-2 acceptances on launch day is fast and positive | Ignore if zero — this is normal on Day 0 |
| BD message replies | Any same-day reply is unusually fast — positive but rare | Ignore if zero on Day 0 |

**The real D-0 signal:** A comment or DM from someone at a target studio or agency is worth more than 1,000 impressions from the wrong audience.

## Response Playbook

### If engagement is strong (comments from relevant contacts by midday)

1. Reply to every comment within 2 hours. Keep it brief: "Thanks — happy to DM if useful."
2. DM anyone who commented from a target company directly. One line: "Saw your comment — are you seeing similar pressure on AI leadership hiring?"
3. At 3pm check: post a brief comment on your own post to push it back into feeds. One observation, not a pitch.

### If nobody engages by 12pm

1. Don't panic — organic reach often peaks 6–24 hours after posting.
2. Comment on your own post with one new thought (this re-triggers LinkedIn's algorithm). Example: add a specific example or a question.
3. At 3pm: DM 2–3 specific connections personally and share the post link directly. Pick people who would genuinely care, not a blast.

### If you receive a negative comment

1. Do not delete it.
2. Reply once, briefly and professionally. Acknowledge their point. Example: "Fair point — happy to discuss in DMs if you want to dig into it."
3. Move to DMs. Do not debate publicly.
4. If it escalates: leave it. One professional response is enough.

## Quick Wins — If It's Going Well

Do these if the post is getting good early traction (strong comments or DMs by midday):

- **Share the post to your connections** — adds a second wave of reach. Only do this if engagement is already happening; sharing a cold post just signals desperation.
- **Tag 2–3 specific contacts in a comment** — choose people you know would have a genuine view. "Interested in your take on this, [Name]." Don't mass-tag.
- **Reply quickly** — LinkedIn's algorithm boosts posts where the author responds to comments early. Every reply you add in the first 2 hours extends reach.
