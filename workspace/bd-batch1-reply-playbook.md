# BD Batch 1 Reply Playbook
*Martyn Makinson | FourPointZero CreativAI | April 2026*

---

Four scenarios. One template each. Personalise in 2 minutes, send within 24 hours.

---

## Scenario 1 — Positive reply

**When to use:** They've replied with genuine interest. Any of these count:
- "Happy to have a chat."
- "This sounds relevant — let's connect."
- "Would love to hear more about this."
- "Good timing actually — we're looking at this space."

**Reply template:**

> [FIRST NAME] — great, really glad this resonated.
>
> I'd suggest a quick 20 minutes to compare notes. I'm available [DAY at TIME UK] or [DAY at TIME UK] — does either work for you?
>
> Happy to use Zoom, Teams, or a call, whichever is easiest.
>
> Martyn

**Next action:** Update tracker to **POS**. Before the call, review `workspace/bd-discovery-call-script-edited.md` and know one specific thing about their company's AI move. If they've already accepted and messaged immediately, treat it as a hot signal — respond the same day.

---

## Scenario 2 — Soft no

**When to use:** They've replied but declined or pushed timing back. Examples:
- "Not the right moment for us."
- "We handle recruitment internally."
- "We're not hiring at the moment."
- "Come back later in the year."

**Reply template:**

> [FIRST NAME] — completely understood, and appreciate you taking the time to reply.
>
> I'll keep an eye on what's developing in your space. If anything specifically relevant to [COMPANY] comes across my desk, I'll flag it. No agenda beyond that.
>
> Martyn

**Next action:** Mark **SOFT** if they said "not right now" or referenced timing. Mark **NEG** if they said no clearly and finally. Set a 6-week calendar reminder. Only re-engage if a new external signal appears — news item, LinkedIn post, open hiring announcement. No signal, no contact.

**If they push back specifically on internal recruitment teams:**

> Completely understood, most companies I work with have strong internal TA for the majority of roles.
>
> The specific difference with senior AI leadership in creative production: this candidate pool is small enough that it doesn't respond to typical sourcing. The people we're placing are in senior roles and move through relationships, not job boards. Worth knowing for when the right role comes up.
>
> Happy to leave it there for now.
>
> Martyn

---

## Scenario 3 — Redirect

**When to use:** They've replied but said you've reached the wrong person, or referred you to someone else. Examples:
- "You'd be better speaking to [Name] — they lead this."
- "This sits with our People team, contact [Name]."
- "I've passed your message to our Head of Production."

**Reply template:**

> [FIRST NAME] — really helpful, thank you. I'll reach out to [REFERRED NAME] directly.
>
> Appreciate you making the connection — good to have you in the network either way.
>
> Martyn

**Next action:** Move quickly. Reach out to the referred contact within 24 hours. Use the introduction as your opening line: "I was speaking with [ORIGINAL NAME] at [COMPANY] who suggested I connect with you directly." That's a warm opener. Update the tracker: add the new contact, mark original contact **REDIR**, note the referred name and date.

**If they've CC'd the referred contact or made an intro directly:**

Reply to the thread including the new contact:

> [NEW NAME] — thanks for making the introduction, [ORIGINAL NAME]. [NEW NAME], looking forward to connecting. I'll send a note separately.

Then follow up with the referred contact in a fresh message using the warm intro framing above.

---

## Scenario 4 — No reply after 5 days

**When to use:** You've sent the connection request and it's been accepted, or you've sent the first message, and 5 days have passed with no response.

**One follow-up template (send on Day 5–7):**

> [FIRST NAME] — just a short follow-up in case the last message got buried.
>
> Happy to connect if AI leadership in creative production is on your radar this year. If the timing isn't right, no problem — worth knowing you're in the network.
>
> Martyn

**Next action:** Send this once. If no reply within a further 7 days, mark **DORMT** and set a re-engagement reminder for 6 weeks out. Only re-engage then if there's a new external signal. Do not send a third touch on LinkedIn — switch to email or park it.

---

## Quick reference

| They do this | Scenario | Tracker |
|---|---|---|
| Reply with interest | Scenario 1 — propose two call times | POS |
| Reply declining or stalling | Scenario 2 — graceful close | SOFT or NEG |
| Redirect to another person | Scenario 3 — thank them, follow the lead within 24 hrs | REDIR |
| No reply after 5 days | Scenario 4 — one follow-up, then park | DORMT |
| Call confirmed | Prep using `bd-discovery-call-script-edited.md` | MTG |

**Update `bd-response-tracker.md` after every reply sent or received.**
