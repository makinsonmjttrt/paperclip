## 2. Tagline Rewrite (3 Variants)

LinkedIn company page taglines: max 120 characters. Displays in search results. This is the SEO headline equivalent.

**Variant A (Category-defining):**
> Executive search for AI leadership in creative production. UK and US.

*Character count: 65. Owns the intersection explicitly. "UK and US" signals geographic reach.*

**Variant B (Problem-forward):**
> Placing the leaders who bridge AI and creative production. Since 2019.

*Character count: 69. "Since 2019" provides tenure proof. "Bridge" signals the gap FPZ fills.*

**Variant C (Audience-specific):**
> Senior recruitment for VFX, virtual production, spatial computing, and AI-integrated studios.

*Character count: 92. Rich in search keywords. No ambiguity about the sectors covered.*

**A/B test recommendation:** Lead with Variant A. It stakes a specific category claim that competitors cannot yet copy. Watch for: profile views from VFX/creative tech company employees in the first 30 days. If views come predominantly from general recruitment audiences rather than creative tech, switch to Variant C, which is more keyword-rich and discovery-oriented.

## 3. About Section Rewrite

**Constraints:** Max 2,000 characters (company page). First 156 characters visible in search previews — write them to function as a standalone hook.

---

**Hook (156 characters, visible in search without clicking):**

> The intersection of AI and creative production is creating roles that most recruiters don't recognise. We do. FourPointZero places senior leaders there.

*Exact character count: 152. Opens with a market observation, not a firm description. Forces the reader to think about a real problem before we name ourselves.*

---

**Full About Section:**

> The intersection of AI and creative production is creating roles that most recruiters don't recognise. We do. FourPointZero places senior leaders there.
>
> We are a specialist executive search firm for the creative production industry. Since 2019, we have placed senior talent at studios, agencies, and platforms working at the point where creative work meets emerging technology — virtual production, real-time rendering, spatial computing, experiential, VFX, and AI-integrated workflows.
>
> Our CreativAI practice focuses on the leadership roles that require both technical AI depth and creative production credibility: Head of AI, CAIO, Head of ML at VFX studios, VP AI Production, Director of AI Strategy. These are cross-market roles that internal TA teams and generalist AI search firms cannot bridge. We can, because we have spent six years building networks inside the production community.
>
> We are members of APSCO, VRARA, and AIXR. We cover the UK and US markets. Every week, we publish a briefing on AI and creative production for senior leaders navigating the transition.
>
> If you are hiring a senior leader for a role that sits at the AI-creative production intersection — or if you are that leader — the conversation starts here.

*Word count: ~220 words. Character count: ~1,340 — well within the 2,000-character limit.*

---

### What This Copy Does

- **Hook:** Opens with a market observation, not a service description
- **Credibility:** Six years tenure, named sectors, named role types
- **Proof:** APSCO, VRARA, AIXR membership; UK and US coverage; weekly newsletter
- **CTA:** One action, two audiences (hiring and candidates)
- **No buzzwords:** Zero instances of the banned terms
