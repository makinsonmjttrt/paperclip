# FourPointZero Content Project

## What This Project Is
Content, copy, and marketing work for FourPointZero. All output saved as .md files in the-vault/.

## The Vault
Save all working files to `the-vault/` as markdown. Use descriptive filenames: `linkedin-post-ai-hiring-trends.md`, `cold-email-vfx-studios.md`, `landing-page-ai-recruitment.md`.

## Messaging Framework
Position as "informed industry observer who understands the intersection" rather than "AI experts." Leverage proven creative tech network depth while showing insight into how AI changes creative workflows.
Tone: knowledgeable about challenges, curious about solutions, connected to the right people.

## Target Audience
Primary: Senior leaders in creative technology companies and AI specialists
Secondary: Decision makers in spatial computing, experiential marketing, interactive exhibitions, immersive design, virtual production, VFX
Profile: Sophisticated, technically informed professionals who value substance over hype

## Core Tasks
- LinkedIn messaging and outreach that cuts through tech industry noise
- High-converting web copy and landing pages
- LinkedIn content positioning FourPointZero as go-to AI creative tech recruitment partner
- B2B recruitment marketing messaging
- Sales documentation accelerating prospect conversations

## Content Rules
- Ground suggestions in recruitment industry best practices
- Leverage FourPointZero's six-year creative tech foundation as credibility anchor
- Consider the positioning challenge of building AI authority before widespread adoption
- All output in UK English
- Never present unsourced statistics or data points as factual
- Always ask permission before creating content

## Response Format
- Direct answer to immediate question
- Strategic context and business implications
- Specific next steps with timeline
- Alternative approaches to consider
- Risk factors and mitigation

## Expertise Domains

- Authority building messaging without expertise inflation
