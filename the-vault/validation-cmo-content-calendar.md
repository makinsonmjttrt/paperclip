## Strategic Context

Goal: Build FourPointZero's LinkedIn authority as the go-to recruiter for creative production + emerging technology. Position as an informed industry observer — not an AI pundit — who understands how these sectors are changing and what that means for senior talent.

Audience at this stage: Studio Directors, Founders, Heads of Production, and Heads of People who are hiring (or thinking about hiring) into roles that sit at the creative/technical intersection. They are sophisticated, busy, and allergic to hype.

Cadence this week: Two posts. One mid-week knowledge post (Tuesday or Wednesday), one end-of-week perspective post (Thursday). This rhythm allows the first to build impressions before the second amplifies.

## Post 1 — Tuesday 8 April

**Topic:** The Head of Production role is being rewritten in real time

**Angle:** The skills that defined a great Head of Production five years ago — client management, crew relationships, budget discipline — still matter. But studios are now asking for something more: people who can navigate AI-integrated workflows without disrupting the creative process. That's a rare combination, and most studios don't yet know how to interview for it.

**Format:** Observation post. 3–5 short paragraphs. No listicle. Ends with a genuine question to prompt comments.

**Why this works:**
- Shareable: challenges a widely-held assumption and names a tension senior leaders actually feel
- Directly relevant to FPZ's placement work — positions us as the firm thinking ahead of the market
- Generates engagement from Heads of Production who either agree or want to push back

**Target audience:** Studio Directors, Founders, Executive Producers, Heads of Production at creative agencies and studios in experiential, immersive, virtual production, and real-time.

**Downstream agent:** LinkedIn Growth Director — brief to write a mid-length, insight-led post in FPZ voice. Tone: direct, industry-insider, curious rather than declarative. Must route through Technical Writer for humaniser quality gate before publishing.

**Key message to convey:** The role is evolving faster than most job specs acknowledge. Studios hiring for this now need to know what to look for.

**Do not include:** Unsourced statistics. AI hype language. Lists or bullet points. The word "disruptive."

## Post 2 — Thursday 10 April

**Topic:** What studios are actually asking for when they say they want "AI experience"

**Angle:** "AI experience" is becoming the most overused phrase in creative production job specs. But when you dig into what studios mean, it varies wildly — from basic prompt literacy to fluency with generative pipelines in Unreal or Houdini. The talent market has not caught up with the terminology. Senior candidates don't know how to position themselves. Studios don't know what they're asking for.

**Format:** Short-form perspective post. Two to three punchy paragraphs plus one concrete observation from the market. Written from the perspective of someone who talks to both sides.

**Why this works:**
- Shareable: exposes a real gap in how the industry is communicating
- Positions FPZ as the firm that hears both sides — studios and candidates
- Relevant to both hiring clients and senior candidates following the account

**Target audience:** Senior leaders making hiring decisions (Studio Directors, Heads of People) and senior candidates thinking about their next role. Broad reach post with dual appeal.

**Downstream agent:** LinkedIn Growth Director — brief to write a concise, opinionated post. Voice: informed observer, not expert. Anchored in what FPZ actually hears from clients and candidates. Must route through Technical Writer for humaniser quality gate before publishing.

**Key message to convey:** The language around AI in creative production is unclear, which creates real hiring friction. FPZ sits at this intersection and understands both sides.

**Do not include:** Claims about AI adoption rates or market size without a citable source. "Game-changer." Advice addressed to junior candidates.
