## Message 1: Connection Request (300 characters max)

> Saw [Company] has been expanding its real-time pipeline — the shift in tooling right now is interesting. I work in recruitment across VFX and creative tech. Would be good to connect.

**Character count:** 181 — within limit.

**Personalisation note:** Replace "[Company] has been expanding its real-time pipeline" with a specific signal when available — a product launch, a recent hire, a press mention, or a post the prospect made.

---

## Message 2: First Message (Send 24-48 hours after accept)

> Thanks for connecting.
>
> I work with VFX studios and creative tech companies placing senior production and technology leadership — specifically people who can bridge the craft side and the AI-integrated pipeline side.
>
> Curious — is the AI tooling strategy something you own directly at [Company], or is it still sitting across a few different functions?
>
> Happy to share what I'm seeing work at studios navigating similar questions either way.

**Format notes:** Short paragraphs. Question drives dialogue, not a pitch. No ask for a meeting in the first message.

---

## Message 3: Follow-Up #1 (Day 7 — no response)

> Saw a post this week from someone at a mid-size VFX studio describing the same challenge: the Head of Technology role is effectively becoming two jobs — one technical, one creative operations.
>
> A few studios are starting to separate those functions as they scale their AI capability. Others are going deep on one person who can hold both.
>
> Curious where [Company] sits on that. No pressure either way — just a question worth asking if you're thinking about it.

**Format notes:** Adds genuinely new context (the bifurcating role observation). Does not say "just following up." Ends with a low-friction question, not a CTA.
