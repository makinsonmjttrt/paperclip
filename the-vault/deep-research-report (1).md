## Why VFX and creative production hiring naturally leans away from retained search

The sector’s hiring culture is demonstrably community-embedded and event-driven, which structurally favours warm access and social proof over formalised retained searches.

The Visual Effects Society presents itself as having over 5,000 members “in 50+ countries”, which signals a global but still socially navigable professional community where reputations travel quickly. citeturn1search32  ACM SIGGRAPH 2025 positioned AI as “a unifying thread” and reported 12,400 attendees from 84 countries, demonstrating how quickly AI-oriented production conversations can concentrate in a few global venues. citeturn1search33

In other words, “networked culture” does not eliminate retained search, it changes when it becomes worth paying for.

You are right that “% of base” and “% of total comp” can create confusion and apparent contradictions.
