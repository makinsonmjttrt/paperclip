# LinkedIn Post: AI and the Creative Director Role in VFX

**Topic:** How AI is changing the creative director role in VFX studios
**Hook type:** Contrarian/Myth-Bust
**Structure:** Problem-Agitate-Solution
**Word count:** ~210 words
**Status:** Draft — awaiting Technical Writer quality gate

---

The title "Creative Director" is quietly splitting into two different jobs.

In VFX studios, AI hasn't made the role smaller.

It's made it more expensive to get wrong.

The CDs thriving right now aren't the ones who've mastered every AI tool.

They're the ones who've decided where AI fits in their creative process — and where it doesn't.

**The role that's disappearing:** Creative Director as craft executor. Someone who signs off on every frame because they know what right looks like.

**The role that's emerging:** Creative Director as systems thinker. Someone who designs the workflow, decides where AI amplifies the team, and owns the creative standard at the output level.

These are fundamentally different people.

Studios trying to fill CD roles with the first profile and hoping they'll adapt to the second are going to have a difficult time.

The technical competence bar has shifted.

So has the management model.

I've spoken with several VFX studios over the past few months who are navigating exactly this.

Some have rewritten the role brief entirely.

Others are still posting the same spec they used in 2022.

The shortlists look very different.

Are you hiring for the role that exists — or the role that existed?
