# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-03-31*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Specialist recruitment for creative production — placing senior talent where creativity meets emerging technology.
**What it does:** FourPointZero finds and places senior leadership talent with studios and agencies working on experiential, interactive, spatial computing, and real-time projects where AI is entering production pipelines.
**Product category:** Specialist/niche recruitment
**Product type:** Service (recruitment agency)
**Business model:** Retained and contingency recruitment fees; established 2019

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Production studios, creative agencies, experiential agencies, immersive tech studios, post-production houses, real-time content studios
**Decision-makers:** Heads of Production, Studio Directors, COOs, Founders, Heads of People/HR
**Primary use case:** Hiring senior production leadership who understand both traditional craft and emerging tech (spatial computing, real-time, AI-integrated workflows)
**Jobs to be done:**
- Fill senior production roles with candidates who can bridge creative and technical
- Reduce time-to-hire in a niche talent market with limited candidate pools
- Access passive candidates who aren't on generalist job boards
**Use cases:**
- Studio scaling for a new XR/immersive division
- Replacing a departing Head of Production or EP
- Building out a team for AI-integrated production workflows
- Hiring senior talent in experiential, interactive, or spatial computing

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Creative production studios can't find senior talent who understand both traditional production craft AND emerging technology (spatial computing, real-time engines, AI pipelines). The intersection is tiny and mainstream recruiters don't know the space.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generalist recruiters don't understand the sector — they send irrelevant candidates
- Job boards attract juniors, not senior passive talent
- Internal hiring teams lack the network in this niche
- LinkedIn recruiting is noisy and time-consuming for specialist roles
**What it costs them:** Roles sit open for months. Projects get delayed. Existing team burns out covering gaps. Studios miss opportunities because they can't staff up fast enough.
**Emotional tension:** Frustration at wading through irrelevant CVs. Fear of making a bad senior hire in a small, reputation-driven industry. Anxiety about falling behind as the sector shifts toward AI-integrated workflows.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other specialist creative/production recruiters (e.g., Aquent, Major Players, The Dots Jobs) — fall short because they cover broader creative industries, not the specific intersection of production + emerging tech
**Secondary:** Generalist tech recruiters who claim to cover "creative tech" — fall short because they don't understand production workflows, craft, or the studio model
**Indirect:** Internal talent teams, LinkedIn Recruiter, referral networks — fall short because the candidate pool is small and passive; you need deep sector relationships to access it

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Sole focus on creative production + emerging technology intersection
- Deep network built since 2019 across experiential, interactive, spatial computing, and real-time
- Understands both the creative and technical sides of production roles
- APSCO, VRARA, and AIXR member — embedded in the industry, not observing from outside
- UK and US coverage
**How we do it differently:** We only recruit in this space. Every conversation, every event, every placement builds a deeper map of who's who in creative production. Generalists dip in and out; we live here.
**Why that's better:** Clients get shortlists of relevant, vetted candidates faster — not a pile of CVs to sift through.
**Why customers choose us:** They've been burned by generalist recruiters who don't understand the space. They want someone who speaks the language and knows the talent.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We prefer to hire through our network" | Your network is finite. Ours extends across the entire creative production sector — including passive candidates you'd never reach. We complement your network, not replace it. |
| "Recruitment fees are too high" | A senior role sitting open for 3-6 months costs far more in lost projects, overtime, and missed opportunities. We typically fill roles faster because we already know the candidates. |
| "We've had bad experiences with recruiters" | So have most studios — because they used generalists. We only work in creative production, which means every candidate we send is relevant. Ask our clients. |

**Anti-persona:** Companies hiring junior/mid-level generalist roles. Agencies outside creative production (pure digital marketing, traditional advertising without production). Companies looking for the cheapest recruitment option.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with generalist recruiters sending irrelevant candidates. Roles sitting open too long. Internal team stretched thin trying to source niche talent.
**Pull:** A recruiter who actually understands creative production. Pre-vetted candidates who've delivered on similar projects. Faster time-to-hire.
**Habit:** Existing recruiter relationships. "We've always used [big agency]." Internal HR processes that default to posting on job boards.
**Anxiety:** "Will this niche recruiter have enough candidates?" "What if they're too small to handle our volume?" "Is the fee justified for a boutique firm?"
